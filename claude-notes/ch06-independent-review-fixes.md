## The consolidated fix: the price now gets a scene
"Nobody asked who someone was" is gone. After *"Someone may have to be in the tunnel the water claims first"*:
1. **Camilla's dents in the table come first,** then she says ***"Not him."***
2. **Gage doesn't deny it.** He remembers Otto's *"I'll go… I'm faster than you,"* now attributed, and says only *"I know the run. That's all I said."* Camilla: *"That's not all you said."*
3. **Ben names the cost at home.** He asks what the street eats when the silver stops. **This answers Fable's question about what Sector One lives on afterwards.** Flitzberg: *"The ration is drawn on the quota board, not on the vein… it does not stop because a wall gives way."* Ben throws Flitzberg's own Ch2 line back at him: *"Somebody always pays… If you're wrong, it's her door."* Flitzberg: *"Then I must not be wrong. And you will watch me, and tell me if I am."* **Ben doesn't say yes.**
4. **This answers Sonnet's question, "why a person and not a sensor?"** Camilla points at the lens in the ceiling: *"You can't put one on a wall?"* Flitzberg: *"Rock they read as noise… A hand on the stone can tell a blow that landed from a blow that didn't. Nothing we have can."*
5. **Camilla claims the workshop:** *"Give me your workshop and whatever you've got that listens, and I'll build you eyes for the wall. Then nobody stands there."* (This echoes Book 1, where her strip gave Gage "her Eyes.")
6. **Flitzberg: *"Then build it. And if it sees, someone may still have to carry it in."*** Her device can cut the risk but not remove it, as the fork asked. Gage asks *"When?"*, and the nineteen nights land straight on her deal. Her metal hand presses down over the dents.

**What later chapters still need from this:**
- **It doesn't settle who stands at the wall.** Ch15's rehearsal still has Gage's finger as the "someone" at the junction, and ch22 still has "who might have to be in the tunnel" open. Ch6 promises an attempt, not an answer.
- **Knock-on for the fork:** Ch6 only claims the job; it doesn't start building or name a device, so ch9's next-day workshop still holds. Beyond that, ch9's workshop scene, and later ch15 or ch18, should show Camilla working on the wall-listener at least once, then succeeding, failing or half-succeeding. Otherwise *"Then build it"* is a dropped thread. I've sent this to the fork; it isn't changed yet.
