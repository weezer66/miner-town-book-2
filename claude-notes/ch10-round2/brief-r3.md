### 1. Give Rohan something to refuse
Elara tells Rohan she left her station and won't say why. Make that the price. Rohan says something close to:
> "Ashoka does not deploy for someone who hides her own breach. Tell me why you stood up, or take your request back to Drona."

**She tells him.** This is a canon decision.
- She tells him what *she* did: she stood up so that a held number would close and fall to one boy. 4055, his father's.
- **She keeps Anandi and the inspection-office clerk out of it.** She doesn't name them and takes it as hers alone.
- Ch13 later has Rohan remember this meeting: "…while Elara told him, with her hands flat and shaking, that she had done it alone." That line must stay true. Let a reader, and maybe Rohan, half-see that she's shielding someone. One beat from him is enough; he doesn't press.

- Then "Put it there… Elara Nathan, Drona." follows, as before. Her name on the request is the second half of the price. Keep Rohan's line "If my people find this, Ashoka hears it before you do. And your name goes on the request." Order the two demands however reads best.
