## The consolidated fix: Drona names a price, and Elara answers
- **"Why me?" now gets a real answer** (Fable's line, adapted). It keeps the guardrail opening the fork asked for: *"Not by destiny, but because you acted while action still mattered. The record carries your name, not ours. There is a line in the Sector One induction file that has been struck through and written again, and beside it an audit reference, and beside that a name. Kubera has read it. Whoever moves against those boys now must move through you. We did not choose you, Monitor. You signed."* This pays off Ch4's deliberate struck record (*REF… NATHAN, E.*), so the forgery becomes her burden instead of being waved off.
- **The price, said aloud:**
  - Drona *won't* take her name off the record.
  - It *won't* lie for her: if Kubera raises it, *"Drona will say what is true, that its monitor signed."*
  - She stays *"the monitor of record for those three boys for as long as this lasts. Every eye that turns toward them will find you first."*
- **Kept at the fork's request:** *"Drona stands behind you. Not in front. Your mentors will be your shadows from today, whatever follows. They cannot carry it for you."*
- **She bargains** in front of them:
  - *"Then the file comes to me… Nothing on those boys goes to any board, from any house, until it has crossed my console."*
  - Sarvajña: *"That is a monitor under review asking to audit her own review."*
  - Elara: *"It's a monitor asking to read what's said about the people she answers for. You just told me I answer for them."*
  - Draṣṭā laughs. The Ancient One: *"Agreed."*
- **It ends on her answer:** her hand comes open over the word FROZEN. ***"I signed," she said.*** The "It was a reckoning" line is gone.
- **The inversion of the Ch5 template:** Kubera gave Daren power and hid the price; Drona names the price first and gives nothing but the file she bargained for. "Reserved for another time" and "Nobody wrote the breach into anything" are cut.
- **Ch8 still works.** Nobody at the table makes her confess (*"Nobody at the table asked what she had done in the vaults. Nobody needed to."*). So ch8's *"I altered the record"* to Anandi and Kavik is still the first time she says it aloud.
