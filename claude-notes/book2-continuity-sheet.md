### The Obsidian Guard (OG) — mechanics as stated so far
- **Origin (Ch23):** After the six days, Kubera commissioned an invisible guard. Kubera wrote the **founder-tier authority**; Drona built the **reader** (a band that reads forearm nerves a heartbeat before the hand moves). It walked for one generation, then the four Grandmasters sealed it in a **box of four parts** and struck its dialect. Drona lied: it kept the plans **and** the Sākṣī mirror channel, which records every *order* the Guard gives.
- **What it does:** It answers every instrument's identity query in that instrument's own dialect. It never refuses to answer, because an unanswered query is an anomaly (Ch17). Doors open before the hand rises; logs read "maintenance interval, no personnel." Through the **Continuity Reserve**, it can move money with no approver (Ch10–11).
- **Its limits:** It is blind to paper, to animals (the **cat**, Ch17) and to anyone with a **bare wrist** (Baju Ch22; Elara and Rohan Ch24). It "cannot make the forgetting itself invisible" (Ch22): every look-away order lands on the Sākṣī wall (Ch23).
- **Fear rule (Sarvajña, Ch23):** "A careful man wearing the Guard is invisible. A frightened man wearing the Guard is a weapon that fires itself." **You will find it when it is afraid.**
- **The ensemble (Ch9):** Layered plates, collar, mesh-lined gloves, hood, sealed boots. The glove produces a short-range vector field. "Your instructor will tell you what you are ready to use" (that instructor has never appeared).
- **Lineage (tracker, decided 2026-10-17):** The Master of the Reserve wore the band before Daren. On the page so far this is only seeded: Ch11 "the man who had taught him to sign them"; Ch14 "the private instruction from the Master of the Reserve"; Ch23 "a careful man… is invisible." **Daren's belief in Ch24 that "the Grandmaster did not know" is Daren's error.** Keep it framed as his inference.

### Patal Loka (Ch14, Ch21)
- A ring beneath the boundary at the old **Interchange Station**, with no central room and no house insignia. It has its own generators, wells and scrubbers. Fewer than 200 people.
- **Chief Architect: Mr. Flitzberg (he).** The first Chief Architect was a woman, a medic from the six days.
- **Standing consent:** what the office requires, it may *take*. Each house's books show it as an ordinary internal transaction.
- **Balance Review:** once a year, at a different place each year. Grandmasters leave their terminals at the first door, then a closed car and an unmarked lift. **"They know they met me. They could not lead anyone to where."** (Ch21)
- **Deterrence:** if the Chief Architect is harmed, a century of ledgers opens to *every representative* of all four houses.
- **Knowledge circle:** the four sitting Grandmasters, each told once in an unrecorded room, plus Patal Loka's own people. The retired Grandmasters stay silent.
- The Dhammapala hints at it twice ("safeguards older than Ashoka", Ch16 and Ch19) and refuses to name it.

## 6. Continuity problems found

### 6.1 Council-day arithmetic (Ch22 vs Ch24) — real, needs a fix
Ch22 fixes the council for **the day after tomorrow** from night N, and Daren thinks "two days had seemed like plenty." Ch24 then has the dead-room rebuild take **three nights** while the council is still **tomorrow**. These can't both hold.
- **Fix A (smallest):** In Ch22, change "day after tomorrow" to **"in four days"** and "two days" to **"four days."** Ch24 then stands as written.
- **Fix B:** In Ch24, change "three nights" to **"one night"** (and probably "four weeks" of logs to something one night of work can cover).

### 6.2 #4055's "whose hand?" already has an answer on the page
Ch10 and Ch22 show the reader that **Elara, Anandi and Kavik** timed the safety inspection so #4055 went to Gage. When Flitzberg asks "Whose hand?" (Ch18, Ch21), the reader already knows: it was Drona's. The tracker says that question resolves to **the Master of the Reserve** at the climax. To make both true, the climax has to separate two layers:
- **Elara's layer:** inspection timing, recent, Drona.
- **The Master's layer:** an older OG irregularity in the same slot batch (Ravi's file). One natural bridge is **Ch1's "someone has been feeding him off the books since childhood."**

Decide which layer Flitzberg's question is really about **before** drafting the climax. Otherwise it reads as the book forgetting Ch10.

### 6.3 "Annex posting" in Ch24 should be the mandate review
Ch24 lines 97, 155 and 177 credit Rohan's Drona-tower access to "the **Annex** review/posting." The Annex is Chanakya's archive. Ch20's postings that put Ashoka inside Drona came from the **mandate review**: "Inspection accompaniments for Drona's monitoring stations." Suggested wording: "the mandate review gave you" and "under his inspection posting."
