# Chapter 25 — Beat Sheet (proposal)

*Companion to `book2-continuity-sheet.md`. Written 2026-10-18 after a full read of Ch1–24.*

A draft, `manuscript/chapter-25-the-door-beneath.md` (about 2,000 words: Otto / Ravi / Elara and the Ancient One), appeared in the folder while this sheet was being prepared. This sheet **keeps that draft's spine** and does three things:
1. lists the continuity breaks in the draft's third section;
2. proposes a reworked version of that section;
3. adds the Daren beat the chapter needs.

Nothing in the draft has been changed.

## C. Proposed structure

**Working title:** keep *The Door Beneath*. It now points at the dead room's locker and at Daren's room, not at Patal Loka. Alternatives: *Another Road*, *Different Light*.

**Epigraph options** (all verbatim; re-verify with the house checker):
1. Keep the draft's: *"the Engine is blind without the Eyes. Not as a saying. As fact."* (Book 1, Ch22). Fits Otto's return.
2. *"The facial-rec runs onboard — the drone recognizes you locally and stores the package, but the upload to Jung only happens when the inspection completes."* (Book 1, Ch7: The Whole Rebellion). Fits Elara: the face is stored locally, and the "upload" waits until a second road confirms it.
3. *"The system had simply stopped recognizing him. He was a man carrying a piece of corporate property welded to his skeleton that he couldn't use and they couldn't be bothered to collect."* (Book 1, Ch14: Phantom Leg). Fits Daren and the band, and echoes Camilla's arm.

**Length:** 4,500–5,500 words, matching Ch21–24. **Dialogue:** about 25–30% (a scene chapter; Ch24 was quieter in the corridor).

### C1. Otto (keep the draft, two small fixes) — about 900 words
- Keep the beats as drafted: hand → wrist → arm → breath → eyes. The medic holds the safe cycle; Gage, Ben and Camilla each say yes; "How long?"; Otto's shaking right hand.
- Fix "three rooms away" to "two levels down."
- **Optional closing line** (it bridges to C3): Otto's first question after "How long?" is about the stones, or "Did they hold?" It answers the trial and tells the reader Otto came back carrying the same burden he went down with.

### C2. Elara — the other road (replaces the draft's Ancient One / Patal Loka section) — about 1,500 words
1. **Dawn.** She wakes from the dream with the shape attached to a name: the face across the blue monitoring room, gold at the collar. *Different light, different coat.* Keep the draft's strongest line: "The dream was not evidence. But the face in it had come from somewhere real."
2. **The rule.** The Ancient One's terms forbid acting on it. So she goes looking for the dullest true thing that could test it.
3. **The record.** She needs to know who the room behind that door belongs to. Two lawful roads, both public-tier:
   - **The tower's guest register for the Sector 1 cycle.** The four visiting monitors' assigned quarters, with room numbers on the fourth level. One of them is the door. Assigned to the Kubera representative for the cycle: **Daren.** Released at cycle end: **never** (Ch9, 11, 17: no one reclaimed it).
   - **Housekeeping's return log** (optional, and stronger). Daren folded his Kubera uniform on the bed "for the house to reclaim" (Ch9). Kubera is "a careful auditor" (Ch5 epigraph: the wrench). The log shows one representative-grade Kubera uniform collected from that room on the morning after his removal, and entered as returned property. **The uniform is the wrench.** Elara gets a paper record that ties the room to the man.
4. **The beat.** She has a name and a room, both on paper, both citable, and neither of them proves a crime. "A man assigned to a room is not a man who killed in a corridor." Keep the rule in force.
5. **No summons.** Drop the call to the Master of the Reserve. Elara asks for the Ancient One through Anandi, on paper, as in Ch23.

### C3. Daren — the morning after the council (new) — about 1,400 words
1. **The tower digest.** Daren reads the Drona tower's overnight security digest the way he reads everything: it reads records, so it reads the record of itself. One line: **two unidentified persons, fourth-level service stair, at his hour; identity queries unanswered; referred to tower security for follow-up.** This is Rohan's Ch24 prediction paying off.
2. **Recognition.** No terminal answered. That's what a bare wrist looks like in a log. The last man with a bare wrist died with his palm on steel. Two of them stood within strides of his door last night and he walked past them.
3. **Fear arrives, and he does nothing on purpose.** This is the Ch23 discipline: hand flat, breath down, band cooling. He can't erase the line (Treasury instinct: an anomaly that vanishes is louder than one that stays), and he can't move rooms (a move is a trail). Every option is an order the band would carry. He doesn't know about the Sākṣī, but the reader does: **any order he gives now lands on the grey wall.**
4. **The Grandmaster's words return.** "You are standing in the wrong place." He hears it again, and for the first time the line could be literal: does the Master know where he sleeps? Keep this **Daren's inference** (per the tracker, the Master knows exactly what the band listens to), and keep it unresolved.
5. **Close.** He opens Baju's notebook to the page where the circles thicken and finds his own door at the center. He realizes the city's blind spots now draw his outline in two hands: Baju's pencil and last night's unanswered queries. *A hand that has to hurry and cannot move.*

### C4. Ravi — the fiber vaults (keep the draft) — about 700 words
- Keep it as written: public gate with his terminal on; the paper index; the author's sheet separated from the sealed finding; the paper custody ledger; "Tomorrow," a promise he's not sure he can keep.
- **The number as an object** (tracker permission): the bare file number on the sleeve, "short, dull, rhymes with 4055 without being it." **Don't** reveal the contents.
- **Optional:** the porter who brings the sleeve up from the stacks is a forgettable man with an unmarked face. Ravi notices him and can't decide if he has seen him before. It's Kaan, but don't say so. This keeps "Room Four. Two hours." alive without converging the hunts.

### C5. Elara and Rohan — the dead room (reworks the draft's last scene) — about 800 words
1. Terminals go into the locker at the door. Paper on the table: the register line and the return-log line. "Daren."
2. **Rohan's beat.** He put his palm on that man's chest once, in a room lit blue (Ch5). Last night he stood four strides from him with empty hands. His own line from the corridor: *"I saw a man who is afraid of his own arm."* Put that next to Sarvajña's rule and Rohan understands the corridor's real danger after the fact.
3. **Then the digest line,** brought by Nayan's runner or read from a tower bulletin anyone can see: the tower is now looking for two unnamed people on the service stair. **The hunters are on the record as anomalies.** Elara's Ch24 cover story ("a Drona reviewer and an Ashoka liaison… simply not wearing our terminals") will soon have to survive questioning.
4. **The decision.** Elara: "What comes after is for the Grandmasters" (her own Ch24 rule). Rohan has to decide what goes to the Dhammapala. Keep the draft's last image, minus the terminal: he looks at the locker, not at a terminal, and the choice stays his.
5. **Possible last line:** something about the room the hand comes home to tonight, now with a name on its door that only two people in the city know.

**Suggested order:** C1 Otto → C2 Elara → C3 Daren → C4 Ravi → C5 dead room. The chapter goes from a body coming back to a name coming back, then to the hand learning it has been seen, and ends with the hunters learning that they have been seen too.

## D. Decisions for the author before drafting

1. **Patal Loka disclosure.** Should Elara learn of Patal Loka in Book 2 at all? If yes, it should come from someone inside the circle (Flitzberg, or a Grandmaster at the climax), not as a briefing.
2. **How does the Ancient One escalate?** Ch14 gives Patal Loka the power to "lay the evidence of a Grandmaster's decision before the other three Grandmasters." The cleanest climax road: the Ancient One goes **around** the Master of the Reserve, to the Dhammapala and the Arthashastri, not to him.
3. **"Room Four. Two hours."** Statement or appointment? If it was an appointment, Ravi has already missed it (Ch24: he went back to his desk), and that miss is a beat in itself.
4. **#4055 layers** (continuity sheet §6.2). Settle these before Flitzberg's "whose hand?" is answered.
5. **Chapters left.** How many chapters until the climax? That decides whether C3's "cannot move" holds, or whether Daren abandons the room in Ch26.
