# Climax plan: v2 Chapters 27–30 (proposal, for the author to decide)

**Budget:** about 16,000 words, four chapters of about 4,000 each.

**Frame:** `structure-v2-two-crews.md` §5a has two fronts on one night.
- **The Lake Wall** (ground): Gage executes, Ben times it, Camilla calls it.
- **The ring:** the out-of-season table; the Master "without escort"; Daren follows.

**The night:** the nineteenth. Daren's flood has Ashoka standing in "every doorway," so the ground is less watched than it has been in a century.

## The proposed split

### 27, "Without Escort" (Camilla · Elara · Daren), about 4,000 words. The night assembles.
- **Camilla (ring control room):**
  - the last check on the listener (it hears rock now, sometimes);
  - Gage and Ben go topside to the Hub under the shift change;
  - Otto insists on staying in the ring, "where the table is";
  - Flitzberg's last words before he goes to sit with four Grandmasters.
- **Elara:**
  - the Ancient One takes her below, blind, "where you can see the work and no one can see you";
  - the four Grandmasters are fetched in closed cars;
  - Rohan comes as Ashoka's escort and is turned back at the first door ("without escort" cuts both ways);
  - Rohan finds his own way down, through the Dhammapala's leave.
- **Daren:**
  - he follows the Master's route, the way the Master "did not need to be taken";
  - the band opens doors that were never on any map;
  - Baju's notebook is against his chest;
  - **the cat image returns**, briefly, as a reflection in a dark lift.
- **End:** the table seated, the Master's rings off, and the first valve on the wall still dry.

### 28, "Feeds" (Camilla · Gage · Elara), about 4,000 words. The Wall front, and the leads meet.
- **The ten minutes:**
  - Ben counts the men off the Hub;
  - Camilla says **"Feeds"**, and Flitzberg's people answer from the ring;
  - the water hammer: Gage at the junction with the listener;
  - "someone may still have to carry it in." The listener works, but only from inside the tunnel.
- **Meanwhile, the ring front breaks:** the Master moves at the table, and the band lights. **Camilla has to choose** which front needs her.
- **Elara meets Camilla** in the control room. Elara is the one who can tell her that the city above isn't watching the wall tonight. The watcher hands the watched a blind spot.
- **D16:** Elara recognizes Camilla's arm as the second signature, and tells her. Camilla learns her arm is watched.
- **End:** the wall goes, and the line to Gage goes quiet.

### 29, "Bare Wrist" (Otto · Daren · Elara · Rohan), about 4,000 words. The ring front.
- **Otto faces Daren (D4):**
  - Otto is stillness; the band has nothing to read ("A man who is not afraid gives the other man nothing to read," ch25);
  - the stop after the correct action;
  - Otto doesn't strike.
- **Camilla reads the band:**
  - the arm and the band are two halves of one invention (D15);
  - the arm lags, the band leads;
  - she knows what it's listening to, and says it aloud.
- **Daren takes the band off himself (D7).** His fingers don't stop this time. This is the book's title image, paid off by its villain.
- **The Master exposed before the four:**
  - Elara's paper road (the register, the custody ledger, Nayan's columns, Baju's notebook from Daren's coat);
  - **Daren as the witness**: "I have a witness," and it is Daren.
- **Rohan nearly breaks** (the door eager to break). **Elara keeps him alive.**
- **Drona's century of watching comes out** in front of the other three.

### 30, "Reckoning" (all), about 4,000 words. The cost and the hooks.
- **The cost at the Wall** (see decision 1).
- **Daren faces Rohan,** Baju's commander. Rohan doesn't forgive him and doesn't strike ("stop when the threat has ended").
- **Plan C:** Ravi's file is opened (see decision 2).
- **The de-keyed band** ends up on Camilla's workbench.
- **Book 3 hooks:**
  - Miner Town's water and power have changed;
  - the balance between the four houses is broken;
  - the crystal's blue quarter is still steady blue;
  - Gretchen at the gates.
- **The last image:** the bare wrists, or the four counting again.

## Decisions needed before drafting

1. **The cost at the Wall:** who carries the listener into the tunnel the water claims first?
   - **(a) Gage, and he survives, badly hurt (recommended).** It answers six chapters of his finger on the split splinter. Loss without killing a lead.
   - **(b) Gage, and the line goes dead at the end of ch28, and he's found alive in ch30.** A bigger cliff, but a reversal.
   - **(c) Someone else.** Gerald from Book 1? Or one of the ring's own people, a cost Flitzberg owes.
2. **Plan C: what Ravi's sealed first-year file is.**
   - **(a) An OG order from three years ago,** before Daren had the band. It's proof the Master wore it, and gives Daren's testimony a second, paper witness. Fits "oldest orders inside Kubera" (ch20). **Recommended.**
   - **(b) Something about the ring:** the first time anyone flagged the forty-day line. Ravi saw the leak first, and the house sealed it to protect the arrangement.
   - **(c) Your own idea.**
3. **How the water hammer lands:**
   - **(a) It works,** and the silver pipeline is broken (the plan's "salvation").
   - **(b) It works too well,** and something of Sector One's is lost too. That's a Book 3 hook.
   - **(c) It partly fails.**
4. **The Master's fate:**
   - **(a) Unseated** before the four Grandmasters, held by Ashoka.
   - **(b) He escapes** for Book 3.
   - **(c) The houses can't agree what to do with him.** That's the broken balance.
5. **How far Patal Loka is exposed:** to the people who were there (per §5a), or wider?
6. **Does Elara meet Camilla in ch28** (control room, recommended), or in ch29?

Once these are settled, I'll write the four briefs, run the two-model drafts in two rounds (27+28, then 29+30), merge them and push, the same way as the earlier rounds.
