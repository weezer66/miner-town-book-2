## Canon constraint (from the fork)
The review suggested aiming the attempt at the radio voice's gap. I didn't, because Ch18 owns that beat: Otto recognises Ben there and "never once picked it up". So the near-escape here is aimed at the quiet sitter and the promise to Gage. Ben isn't named, and "drank" isn't used. The twitch stays inside Otto's experience, and no monitor is mentioned. Ch15 fixes the count at the second logged move, so a logged move here would break it.

**From Fable:** the near-escape passage, which:
- opens "The one that stayed was there again";

I also added Fable's "He could feel it through the blue, one side of him warmer than the other." to the sitter line, as the hook for the attempt.
