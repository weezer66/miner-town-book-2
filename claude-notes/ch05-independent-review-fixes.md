## The consolidated fix: the surrender is now the mechanism
- **Daren asks why.** After *"It was only the first thing I needed you to give me,"* he asks: *"Needed. For what?"*
- **The Master answers:**
  - *"What is in this will not close on a hand the city is still holding. It has to be let go of first."* The wording is deliberately loose, as the fork asked, so the canon that the Master wore the band before Daren still works: he must once have let his own hand go, and Daren doesn't ask.
  - Stripping Daren would have left a record (*"a hand that was taken is still on record, as taken"*). *"A hand that is given leaves nothing behind it."*
- **The humiliation becomes deliberate.** Daren: *"You let them laugh. You played it for me. You wanted me to have nothing left to keep."* Master: *"A man who is asked for his hand bargains for it. I could not ask."*
- **The box was waiting** (*"it had been set out before Daren ever reached the doors"*). The reviewer's "it was a test" reading is now on the page.
- **Daren refuses once.** *"Then no. Whatever that is. No."*
  - The Master lets him go: *"The door will open… the city will forget you by the end of the week."*
  - Daren goes back to the doors. He stands with his bare wrist at the release *"long enough for the release to time out under his fingers,"* then turns around. The turn happens through action, and *"Every broken thing in him turned that way at once"* is cut.
- **Later chapters still fit:**
  - It ties together the existing lines about fighting "what refuses to enter a record", power "with no mark on it", and "no record that can be followed".
  - It fits ch14's *"no one can revoke what he cannot name"* and ch20's *"Without it he was a man with no house, no name."*

## A plant for the canon rule "the Master wore the band before Daren"
After the clasp closes: *"It is cold the first night," the Master said, as if mentioning the weather. "After that you will stop noticing."* Later that night: *"The Master had been right about the cold."* Daren doesn't ask how he knows. A rereader will catch it.

## The Daren–Camilla mirror (the reviewer's suggestion)
It's now explicit in what the band does, though neither character knows about the other:
- Daren's fingers *"sprang open on their own"*;
- *"something closed with them"* under the band;
- "It will learn you faster."

These echo Camilla's arm in ch3 (*"She hadn't told it to"*). It's left implicit, so it doesn't tip the D16 reveal.
