# Ch24 Bare Wrists: YA review

**POV:** Ravi → Elara → Daren → Elara · **Length:** ~4,300 words · **Dialogue:** ~21% · **Job:** the second slip; the plan; the council vote; the corridor watch; the half-seen face

## What already works for YA
- **Taking off the terminals** ("It feels like going deaf." / "It feels like the dead room… Only we are carrying it with us.") speaks straight to a teen reader's relationship with their phone. This may be the most YA-resonant scene in the book. Lean into it.
- **"Have you ever been outside without it?" "No." "Then that is the part you have not thought about yet."** Lovely.
- **The hand is ordinary,** "like someone carrying a full cup." It's a great anticlimax that's actually a climax.
- **The Grandmaster's "your hand is wrong"** is chilling, and the reader knows more than Daren does.
- **Rohan unarmed by choice.** Great character logic.

## Gaps
1. **The Elara–Rohan corridor wait is the closest these two have ever been** ("the warmth of Rohan's shoulder an inch from hers"), and the chapter doesn't let either of them notice it. YA readers will.
2. **Ravi's opening section is all inner columns.** He discovers the slip and then thinks for ~800 words.
3. **⚠ "Annex posting"** should be the mandate review (continuity sheet §6.3), and **"three nights" vs Ch22** (continuity sheet §6.1).
4. **⚠ Rohan predicted the sensors would flag them** ("someone will be sent to find out why"), and the chapter doesn't pay that off. Ch25 doesn't either, so far.
5. **The last line**, "That was not the face of a thing that could not be caught.", is close to the "Not X" construction, and it's a little abstract for an ending this concrete.

## Suggested changes
- **High: one beat of awareness in the recess.** Not romance, just awareness. For example: she can hear his breathing now that nothing else is speaking, and she realizes she has never heard another person breathe without a terminal between them. That's more intimate than any touch, and it fits the bare-wrist theme exactly.
- **High: cut Ravi's two-column reasoning by ~40%.** Keep "A man who copies a thing wants it. A man who leaves it where it is has already got it" and "An instrument that could not call for help was not protected. It was simply alone, with everyone's hands on it."
- **Medium: plant the sensor flag on the way out.** As they reach the service stair, a ceiling reader pulses amber twice at two people it can't name. Rohan sees it; Elara doesn't. One sentence, paid off in Ch25 or 26.
- **Medium: end on the concrete:** *"And somewhere behind it, in a place she could not reach tonight, a half-seen face was waiting to be remembered."* Cut the final line.
- **Low: "Ten strides. Eight."** Good. "Six strides past them" is good too. Keep the counting.

## Keep
"Bare, we are two people with eyes. That is all we are." That's the chapter title's meaning in a line.
