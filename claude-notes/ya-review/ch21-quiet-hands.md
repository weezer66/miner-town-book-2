# Ch21 Quiet Hands: YA review

**POV:** Gage → Flitzberg · **Length:** ~4,900 words · **Dialogue:** ~49% · **Job:** Otto's tank; Camilla's arm; the century film; three conditions; #4055 isn't chance; the mission

## What already works for YA
- **This is the book's most YA chapter,** and its best. The teen cast is on stage, asking sharp questions, setting terms with an adult and winning them.
- **Camilla's arm:**
  - it lags half a breath behind her;
  - it grips Gage so he can't pull back;
  - "Lost an arm. Found one."
  - "I'll learn it first. I'm faster."

  Readers will love her here.
- **The three conditions,** especially Camilla's ("I don't work with anything I'm not allowed to take apart"), and Flitzberg's "There is a workshop two levels down. It has been waiting for someone like you…"
- **The man who said no forty years ago,** with flowers on his grave. A quiet, devastating answer to Ben's question.

## Gaps
1. **⚠ Book 1 check: which arm.** Book 1 (Ch24) says Camilla lost her **left** arm. Ch21 never says which side the metal arm is. Name it once ("the left, where the laser had taken the old one") to lock continuity.
2. **⚠ Book 1 check: Book 1's facility had 20 dormitory beds** and the strange bed bands with a wrist injection that "stayed in them." Ch21 has two-bed rooms and never mentions the injection. If the injection matters later, plant it here (Camilla would ask).
3. **Flitzberg's "three watches" answer is ~400 words.** It's well done, and it's the second time the reader has heard it (Ch14). If Ch14 is cut (see the Ch14 note), this becomes the first time, which is better.
4. **Ben's sister is never named in Book 2.** Book 1 names her **Gretchen**. Use the name; it's a gift to returning readers.
5. **The mission is spoken offstage** ("Then he told them. It took eleven minutes."). That's a good withholding device, but give readers one tiny tell, so the secret feels concrete.

## Suggested changes
- **High: name the left arm** and **name Gretchen** (Ben: *"Gretchen'll be at the gates every day until somebody tells her to stop."*).
- **High: contractions for the teens.** Gage's and Ben's lines are close to Book 1's register already; push them all the way there. Leave Flitzberg formal; the contrast characterizes him.
- **Medium: one Book 1 callback in the workshop exit,** e.g. Camilla asks for her machete, or for Pappy's Master Scripts, or whether the drone lens she kept is still in her pocket. Book 1 readers will feel it.
- **Medium: give the mission one tell.** Ben goes still at the word "salvation" and whispers *"water"*, or Gage's hand finds where the wrench should be. (Book 1's unfinished Lake Wall / water-hammer plan is the obvious candidate. See the index.)
- **Low: the wall film** gives Camilla checking dates and Ben counting faces, which is perfect. Add Gage's eyes on the door one more time *after* the film. It's a small bridge to "Who watches you?"

## Keep
"You will notice that nothing in that answer is trust. It is fear, arranged carefully, pointing in four directions at once."
