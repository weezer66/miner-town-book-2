# Ch18 The Fourth Instance: YA review

**POV:** Elara/Rohan → Flitzberg → Daren · **Length:** ~7,000 words (the longest chapter) · **Dialogue:** ~51% · **Paragraphs over 150 words:** 9 · **Job:** the dead room; the four specialists; the Annex as fourth instance; the trials seen from the glass; the Master and Daren

## What already works for YA
- **The four specialists with code names** (eye, arm, ear, tongue) is the most YA-friendly invention in the book. It's a heist crew with archetypes, pronunciations and rules ("a body has one will"). Readers will pick favorites.
- **Nayan correcting "he" to "it"** is a great, sharp moment that changes how the team works.
- **Camilla's softening sealant on the climb,** seen from the examiners' side ("a burden no one can lift is not shared by announcing it"), adds a new layer to Book 1's best scene.
- **"Whose hand?"** is a strong hook.

## Gaps
1. **⚠ Book 1 contradiction: the "fifth room."** Ch18 says the four doors were done in order and "now the fifth room, the one with no label on its door" held the volcano. In Book 1 (Ch25), the volcano simulation happens **inside the SACRIFICE chamber**, the fourth door. Book 1 also reveals the four rooms were **one room the whole time** (Camilla's blood on the crystal). Ch18 also treats SACRIFICE as already annotated before the simulation began.
2. **⚠ Book 1 contradiction: "the small one thinks in patterns" = Ben.** Ch19 then calls Otto "the small boy who had lowered ten stones." Book 1 has Otto as the small, frail one (5'9" in Book 2 Ch1, Ben 5'8"). Pick one "small one."
3. **The introductions are long speeches.** Each of the four specialists gets a 100–150-word speech from Rohan. The concept is fun; the delivery is a briefing.
4. **Rohan's assignment speech is ~600 words in one paragraph.** It's the longest spoken paragraph in the book.
5. **The Daren and Master scene** runs ~2,000 words of strategy (routing law, inconclusive files). The best beat in it, Daren keeping "one-third of the truth," is buried at the end.

## Suggested changes
- **High: fix the trial structure to match Book 1:**
  - SACRIFICE *is* the Unreal Reality, so delete "fifth room."
  - Rewrite the annotation list so SACRIFICE is the room in progress on the deep feed, and its annotation is the one being *written now*.
  - Optionally, Flitzberg can note the trick the candidates figured out: *"The small one has worked out that it is one room. He always does, eventually."*

  This keeps the scene and makes it match Book 1.
- **High: introduce the specialists through action, not speeches.** Each one does a single thing in the room as Rohan names them:
  - Nayan is already copying the wall onto paper;
  - Baju is checking the door seal with his palm;
  - Kaan is someone Elara didn't notice was standing there until he's named;
  - Jeebh cracks a joke to break the tension.

  Then Rohan gives one line each. This cuts ~400 words and makes them memorable.
- **High: break Rohan's assignment speech into four short exchanges,** one per specialist, with each answering in a line. Let Jeebh be funny ("Three sub-audits. I'll make them so dull I fall asleep filing them.").
- **Medium: cut the Daren and Master scene by ~35%.** Keep:
  - the rings off;
  - "I taught the doctrine to a treasury officer. I am hearing it back from something else";
  - the Ashoka design (*a shield in every doorway*);
  - "Second only to mine."
- **Low: Flitzberg calls Camilla by name** against the examiners' discipline. That's lovely; keep it.

## Keep
"A body has one will. If it had two, it could be pulled apart at the joint." And "I am the only door in this body."
