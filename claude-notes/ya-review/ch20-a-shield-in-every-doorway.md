# Ch20 A Shield in Every Doorway: YA review

**POV:** Daren → Ravi → Daren → Rohan · **Length:** ~4,800 words · **Dialogue:** ~2% · **Job:** the mandate review weapon; Ravi in Room Four; the flood; the Dhammapala's note

## What already works for YA
- **Ravi's Room Four session** works as a heist in reverse: he closes every escape route in a document. And **he leaves one door open on purpose.** It's the first time Ravi resists the hand, quietly, and it's a strong secret for the reader to carry.
- **The Dhammapala's paper slip** ("A flood is also a current… You hold the path."), and Rohan burning it in a true flame. Great.
- **"Wherever the flood pushed hardest — that was the direction to walk."** Strong chapter-ender.

## Gaps
1. **This is the most bureaucratic chapter in the book.** It covers charter interpretation, allocation-register deferral clauses, council tabling, prerogative edges, and eleven clerical fixes. Adult political-thriller readers will enjoy it. YA readers will skim the whole Ravi section unless it has stakes they can feel in the body.
2. **Daren's opening ~1,200 words** are the bracelet hacking Ashoka's roster, explained in close mechanical detail for the third time in the book (after Ch17 and Ch20's own earlier lines).
3. **Ravi's fear is stated but not felt.** He sits with his hands flat and weighs options.
4. **⚠** "the guest quarters Kubera kept for him" contradicts Ch11/17. See continuity sheet §6.4.

## Suggested changes
- **High: cut Daren's roster-hack section by half.** Keep "a knock from inside," the seal flickering from Drona to Ashoka, and "whether he was using it, or whether it had… learned the way he moved." That last line sets up Ch22's death.
- **High: compress Ravi's four closures** to one paragraph of summary each, two sentences apiece. Spend the saved words on his body:
  - his pen hand cramping;
  - him checking the door glass when someone passes;
  - him nearly closing the last gap and stopping.

  The open-door choice is the scene's climax; give it a full beat of physical hesitation.
- **Medium: make the stakes concrete for Ravi.** He imagines one specific Ashoka officer, maybe a responder from the maglev in Ch12, pulled off a real emergency to stand guard at a ceremony. One image makes the policy hurt.
- **Medium: Rohan's section.** The eleven postings are good. Add one Little Dragons beat: he has to cancel a class, and Claudia's brown belt is on the board's list of cancellations. It's small and it stings.
- **Low: "eleven"** shows up again: eleven characters, eleven small things, eleven postings. See the index note on number repetition.

## Keep
"The gap was the price of the disguise." "He left it open. He told himself it was craft." Those are Ravi's best lines in the book.
