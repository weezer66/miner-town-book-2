# Ch25 The Door Beneath: YA review

**POV:** Otto → Ravi → Elara · **Length:** ~2,230 words · **Dialogue:** ~30% · **Job:** Otto wakes; Ravi's folio; Elara names Daren; the Ancient One escalates

> The full continuity review is in `claude-notes/chapter-25-review.md` (Patal Loka location, the call to the Master, the terminal in the dead room, and more). This file covers the YA lens only.

## What already works for YA
- **Otto's waking**, hand to wrist to arm to breath to eyes, is tender and earned. The three of them each saying yes before the tank opens pays off Gage's Ch21 condition beautifully.
- **"How long?" "Long enough."** Good.
- **Short sentences, fast pace.** At 10.7 words per sentence this is the book's fastest prose, and it reads more like YA than any chapter since Ch1.

## Gaps
1. **The reunion is restrained to the point of flatness.** Gage puts his palms on the glass, Ben stands at his shoulder, Camilla stops a step away. Nobody laughs, cries or touches Otto. These are four teenagers who nearly lost one of their own. Book 1's crew would be louder.
2. **Otto says one line.** He's the chapter's first POV and he comes back nearly mute.
3. **Ravi's section is efficient but cold.** His most important act so far gets no physical tell.
4. **Elara's section** turns on a dream and then a briefing. She receives information; she doesn't do anything.

## Suggested changes
- **High: let the reunion be messy.** For example:
  - Ben laughing and crying at once;
  - Gage refusing to let go of Otto's shoulder;
  - Camilla making a joke about the metal arm being "the second-ugliest thing in the room" so Otto can laugh, then wincing because laughing hurts him.

  One touch, one joke, one tear. That's the YA payload of this chapter.
- **High: give Otto a second line, about the stones.** *"Did they hold?"* It tells us he came back carrying the same burden he went under with, and gives Gage the line *"You held them."*
- **Medium: one physical tell for Ravi** as he reads the folio: his thumb pressing the old ink, or his breath stopping. (Keep the contents hidden, per Plan C.)
- **Medium: give Elara one active choice.** The beat sheet suggests the guest-register road. Even a one-paragraph lookup before the summons changes her from someone who dreamed a name to someone who found one.
- **Low: the chapter is short.** If you add the reunion beats (~300 words) and Elara's lookup (~150), it stays brisk and lands at ~2,700.

## Keep
"Camilla said yes last." Small, perfect characterization.
