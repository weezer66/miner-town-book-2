# Ch23 The Proof: YA review

**POV:** Elara → Daren → Otto → medic coda · **Length:** ~7,000 words · **Dialogue:** ~37% · **Paragraphs over 150 words:** 6 · **Job:** Elara takes charge; the Sākṣī and OG origin; the live wall; Daren's tremor; Otto fights his way back

## What already works for YA
- **Elara taking charge:** "Then we are not going to do it again." / "Lead." This is the hero's turn, and it lands.
- **The live wall** ("The hand is up there now. Right now. It is walking."), with the Guard working in "the ugliest font in the city," is a great reveal moment.
- **Otto and Master Chan:** "It is not time to adapt, son. It is time to fight." It inverts Book 1's "Adapt, son. Adapt." Fans of Book 1 will feel this one. The one-finger fight is the emotional climax of the book so far.
- **Daren's tremor** and "nothing at all, on purpose" give a villain inner life. YA readers like a villain who's afraid.

## Gaps
1. **The Guard's origin is told across ~1,100 words** of Ancient One and Sarvajña exposition, in alternating speeches. The lore is good and the delivery is a lecture.
2. **Elara and Rohan's grief beat for Baju is brief.** "Elara did not say she was sorry" is right for them, but the reader never sees Rohan *feel* it. He pinches out a flame, and that's all. Baju was a crew member readers met; give them a moment.
3. **Daren's Meru section repeats his guilt three ways:**
   - the boot;
   - "numbers that were people";
   - "only the absence of anyone able to tell him no."

   That's good material, said twice over.
4. **Book 1 check:** Otto's memory of "a yard behind a shed" matches Book 1 Ch3 ("shed"), not Ch24 ("yard behind the dojo"). Good choice. Keep the shed.

## Suggested changes
- **High: break the origin lore into a Q&A Elara drives.** She asks the short, sharp questions ("Who built it?" "Why seal it?" "Then why does it still work?"), and the elders answer in two or three sentences each. The same information plays as an interrogation, with the teen-adjacent hero asking and the elders on the back foot. It also fixes Ch8's passive-Elara problem by contrast.
- **High: a Baju grief beat for Rohan.** One moment, before "Lead.": *"He had a counter," Rohan said. "He timed everything. He'd have known to the second how long it took me to read the notice."* That's three lines of private memory, and it makes the death land for readers.
- **Medium: cut Daren's Meru section by ~30%.** Keep:
  - the boot;
  - unclenching the hand one finger at a time;
  - "including the things his hand told it that he never heard."
- **Medium: the medic coda is lovely.** Add one detail tying it to the friends: she glances at the three names on the panel, the people who asked to be woken.
- **Low: "Drona lied."** That's two words and a great line. Make sure nothing in the speech after it softens it.

## Keep
"It is not a blank. It never was. It is a signature." That's Rohan's best line.
