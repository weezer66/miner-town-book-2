# Ch9 The Dark Between Stars: YA review

**POV:** Daren · **Length:** ~3,550 words · **Dialogue:** ~16% · **Job:** the second chance; the obsidian band; the black hole; the ensemble; "I will create the ripple."

> Decision log: chronological structure, flashback frame deleted (2026-09-25). Kept.

## What already works for YA
- **A power-up scene for the villain.** The box, the black bracelet, the clasp "louder than thunder." This is superhero-origin territory, and YA readers love it. The bottle floating into his glove is the first time the book is *fun*.
- **The black hole between the stars** is a gorgeous, memorable image, and it recurs well in Ch13 and Ch14.
- **Workers dropping their eyes in the corridor** shows the power socially, not just mechanically.

## Gaps
1. **The opening recaps Ch6's ending** ("The audience should have been over… had surrendered the Kubera bracelet…"). Readers just read it.
2. **"Second only to mine" and "Omnipresent" / "Unavoidable"** are good, but the Grandmaster's speeches about records and naming are long and abstract for a scene that should feel like temptation.
3. **The ensemble calibration is quick**, and then "For more than thirty minutes, they spoke beneath the dark window… No target was named." The reader is shut out of the most interesting conversation.
4. **Daren's interior** is mostly "something settled in him like metal into a mold." We don't get his voice: no glee, no fear, no sarcasm.

## Suggested changes
- **High: open on "You have been marked."** Cut the three recap paragraphs above it.
- **High: let Daren enjoy the bottle.** One beat of disbelieving delight, maybe a second try with something heavier that fails, so it has rules and limits. YA readers like learning a power's rules ("What else can it do?" "More than you can use safely").
- **Medium: give Daren one line of his own humor or bitterness** in the chamber. For example, when told "You will have no title": *"I had a title yesterday. It didn't stop anyone laughing."*
- **Medium: show 2–3 lines of the "thirty minutes" conversation** instead of summarizing it. Even an inaudible fragment works: the Grandmaster saying "Drona first" would plant Ch11.
- **Low: "The sound was quiet. It was also louder than thunder."** Keep it, but it's the third paradox-pair in the chapter. Trim another to protect it.
- **Watch:** the instructor ("Your instructor will tell you…") never appears in Book 2. Either cut the line or plan to pay it off.

## Keep
Leaving the folded Kubera uniform "for the house to reclaim" is a perfect small act, and it can pay off (see the Ch25 beat sheet).
