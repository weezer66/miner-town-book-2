# Ch3 Sanjeevani: YA review

**POV:** Elara (long flashback), then a Rohan coda · **Length:** ~4,900 words · **Dialogue:** ~2% · **Job:** Elara's wound: Teresa, Iris, the descent at 15

> Decision log: compressed in place, kept as one chapter (2026-09-25). The notes below respect that; none of them asks for a split.

## What already works for YA
- **Teresa's first bleed** (the sunrise light, the crimson pillow, red paint on Elara's fingertips) is the most emotionally powerful image in the book's first act. YA readers will remember it.
- **Fifteen-year-old Elara stowing away in a coal pod** to find a mythical herb is pure YA courage. Grief-driven, reckless, and it costs her everything.
- **Iris and the strap**, and Sen turning the monitor around: "your body confessed before your pen did." A great teen-in-a-system moment.

## Gaps
1. **Almost no dialogue for 4,900 words.** The flashback is narrated summary ("Elara became obsessed…", "Elara read medical journals…"), so the reader hears *about* the friendship but rarely hears Teresa speak. YA runs on voice, and Teresa never gets one.
2. **Ages 5–12 move quickly and abstractly** (Vishnu/Voss philosophy, the Institute's mission statement). The reader wants Teresa, and the parents' doctrine delays her.
3. **The ending tells, twice:** "It was not concealment. It was disclosure with better timing" and "a vow… colder than anger." The rewrite plan (§2.8) flagged the thesis paragraph.
4. **The Rohan coda** at the end is a second POV in a chapter that belongs to Elara's grief. It dilutes the landing.

## Suggested changes
- **High: give Teresa four or five spoken lines.** It doesn't need to be scenes, only lines dropped into the summary. For example, during the carbon-dating years: *"Every fragment's a witness, El. They can't stamp a date on decay."* And the promise: *"Stay. Promise me you'll stay."* (That's the promise Elara breaks, so let the reader hear it.)
- **High: one exchange in the hospital,** two or three lines, the night of the water cup. This is the scene readers will cry at, if they're allowed to hear it.
- **Medium: compress ages 5–8** (Vishnu/Voss teaching) to two short paragraphs. Keep the karma definition, because it pays off in Ch7.
- **Medium: the descent to the ground.** Add one sensory beat that's hers alone: the smell, the first child she sees sorting stone. Right now it's a list of conditions.
- **Medium: move the Rohan coda** to the start of Ch4, or cut it. His "threat vs. pillar" read on Otto is good; it just isn't Ch3's ending.
- **Low: cut "It was not concealment. It was disclosure with better timing."** It explains the act; the next paragraph already shows it.

## Keep
The Iris → strap → three linked signals echo in the closing image. It's the kind of pattern rhyme the series does well.
