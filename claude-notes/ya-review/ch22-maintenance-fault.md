# Ch22 Maintenance Fault: YA review

**POV:** Elara → Daren → Rohan · **Length:** ~5,000 words · **Dialogue:** ~0% · **Job:** the boys gone; Camilla's strip; Baju's death; the notebook; Rohan stops waiting

## What already works for YA
- **Baju's death is the turning point of the book,** and it's done brilliantly: Daren didn't decide, his fear did, and the band finished the wish. "He had not opened that door. He had only been afraid in front of it." This is a villain beat with real moral horror, and teen readers will feel it.
- **The boot half off** is a perfect small human detail.
- **Elara reading Camilla's file** ("Camilla's had worked") draws a lovely parallel to her own descent at fifteen. It's the first real bridge between Elara and the ground cast.
- **"No notebook."** Rohan finding the absence is a great detective beat.

## Gaps
1. **Zero dialogue across 5,000 words.** For a chapter with a death in it, that's a lot of interior. The Elara section in particular is a file read.
2. **The Elara section (~1,700 words) comes first** and delays the chapter's real event. A YA reader starts it expecting action and gets records.
3. **Daren's reaction runs long.** The "who did this" reasoning restates the bracelet mechanics from Ch20 and Ch17.
4. **⚠ Council-day arithmetic.** "Day after tomorrow" conflicts with Ch24. See continuity sheet §6.1.
5. **Rohan's "stop listening" message** is ambiguous on first read: is it "stop listening" or "Stop. Listening."?

## Suggested changes
- **High: reorder.** Open with Daren in the corridor (the bare wrist, the eyes, the line test), then Rohan reading the notice, then Elara's file work last. Her discovery that Camilla went with the boys becomes a quiet coda after the violence, and it ends the chapter on the ground cast's resilience. *(Alternative: keep Elara first but cut her section to ~900 words.)*
- **High: give Elara one human exchange.** A clerk at the household archive who remembers the shack-rows, or Anandi checking on her. Two or three lines, so the section isn't solitary.
- **Medium: cut Daren's post-death reasoning by a third.** Keep the hand-on-the-band beat (pulling it, fingers stopping, remembering the pale wrist). That's the scene. Cut the restatement of how the bracelet reads nerves.
- **Medium: make Rohan's message unambiguous.** For example: *"…and the message was two words: stop listening. Start."* Or name the instruction outright.
- **Low: Camilla's ration card stopping** is a great clue. Consider letting Elara also see that the reagent strip's chemistry is Heights salvage, so the reader feels Camilla's cleverness and not only her courage.

## Keep
"He could make every record forget him. He could not make the forgetting itself invisible." That's the book's thesis about power.
