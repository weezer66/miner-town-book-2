# Ch11 The Weakening: YA review

**POV:** Elara → Daren → Elara → Daren → Rohan · **Length:** ~3,500 words · **Dialogue:** ~0% · **Job:** "quiet before the storm"; weaken with abundance; OG = Obsidian Guard; the paper letter

> Decision log: kept at 0% dialogue on purpose ("the aloneness is the dramatic irony"; revisit if beta readers stall here). These notes don't ask for dialogue. They ask for tightening and one concrete beat per POV.

## What already works for YA
- **Parallel night** (hero and villain awake at the same hour, each reaching for an ally) is a classic YA structure, and the mirror is clean.
- **"OG. Obsidian Guard."** The reader gets the answer while Elara doesn't. Satisfying dramatic irony.
- **Rohan's 4 a.m. horse stance**, and a letter slid under the door. A great character entrance into the plot.

## Gaps
1. **Daren's section re-explains the library gift twice:**
   - "Drona would reject a bribe it recognized…"
   - "That was where the weakening began…"
   - "Drona called itself the conscience of the city…"

   That's three paragraphs making one point.
2. **Daren's "fear lived in things Kubera could take"** is a strong idea, but it's told. We never see him test it.
3. **Elara's section is all feeling** (breath short, knees pulled up). It works, but it's short on anything specific.
4. **The "lantern in a scarred open palm" paragraph** is author-voice, outside both POVs.

## Suggested changes
- **High: cut Daren's library section by ~35%.** Keep the requisition-ledger header (PRIORITY: HIGH / NEGLIGIBLE, a great bit of evidence) and "Drona called itself the conscience… He needed to make it busy explaining why the gift was righteous."
- **Medium: one physical test of Daren's fearlessness.** For example, he walks past a Kubera guard who saw his removal and holds the man's eye until the guard looks away, and Daren feels nothing at all. It shows his new state in two sentences.
- **Medium: one specific object for Elara.** She takes out the old Sanjeevani notebook (Ch3) and doesn't open it. That links her current dread to her old one with no explanation needed.
- **Medium: cut or reassign the lantern-image paragraph.** If you keep it, make it Elara's thought, in her terms.
- **Low: Rohan's Dhamma paragraph** is long. The "eight words" callback is lovely; trim the definition of the duty.

## Keep
The reveal that the room register is stamped AUTHORITY: OFFICE OF THE GRANDMASTER, DRONA, with "Someone far above her had quietly agreed to hold it open." It's a perfect end beat.
