# Ch16 The Chamber of Spirits: YA review

**POV:** Rohan · **Length:** ~3,950 words · **Dialogue:** ~18% · **Job:** the ten statues; the three tests (bamboo, steel, Vayun); the charge; four investigators

## What already works for YA
- **The three tests** (bamboo by hand, steel plate, sparring the master) are a martial-arts set piece with a clear meaning per test. The armor plate splitting three seconds after the cut is a great image.
- **The Dhammapala laughing** reveals the woman beneath the title.
- **"Why did you step forward?"** A perfect mentor koan.

## Gaps
1. **The ten statues** come as a catalog. Four get full sentences, then six are compressed. For a YA reader it's a history lesson in the middle of a scene.
2. **The tests are told as remembered,** framed as Rohan replaying them over dinner. The memory frame costs immediacy. These were the most exciting moments in Ch15–16, and we're one step removed from them.
3. **The Grandmaster's "new power" speech** recaps the three instances the reader has already watched.
4. **Rohan blushes and goes quiet** at Vayun's praise. Charming, but Rohan speaks only about six lines all chapter.

## Suggested changes
- **High: move the tests to the end of Ch15, live.** Ch15 ends with "the ceremony had something waiting for him." So show the bamboo, the steel and the spar there, in real time. Ch16 then opens at the bronze doors. Ch15's word count rises; offset that with Ch15's reflection cuts.
- **High: cut the statues to five** in Ch16, and let Rohan react to one personally. For example, he stops at Kusunoki Masashige ("kept his duty knowing the cause was already lost") and the reader feels why.
- **Medium: cut the recap** of the three instances from the Grandmaster's speech. Keep "A power that can be explained away at every step does not have to hide."
- **Medium: give Rohan one question** back to the Dhammapala that shows his mind. For example: *"And if the safeguards older than Ashoka are part of the problem?"* (This sets up Patal Loka's private-accountability weakness from Ch14.)
- **Low: Rohan's unfinished letter** to Elara at the end is never sent in later chapters. Either have Ch18 open with him handing it to her, or cut it.

## Keep
"Dhamma does not wait its turn." That's the Grandmaster's best line.
