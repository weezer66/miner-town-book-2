# Ch5 The Altered Record: YA review

**POV:** Elara · **Length:** ~2,750 words · **Dialogue:** ~6% · **Job:** the heist; Daren's accusation; Rohan's pledge

## What already works for YA
- **A twenty-minute heist with a clock.** It's the best-structured tension in the first act. YA readers love a competent heroine beating a system with its own rules: "The system had been built to distrust people. It had never learned to distrust itself."
- **The struck-through record diff** is a great visual on the page.
- **Rohan's hand on Daren's chest**, and "Whatever happens next, I will stand with you." This is the moment the Elara–Rohan bond starts, and it's earned.

## Gaps
1. **The first three paragraphs are interior debate** (two lawful roads, treason, Teresa's pattern) before she moves. The decision is right; it's just slow.
2. **The archivist passing her in the corridor** is the only moment of human danger, and it lasts two sentences. That's the scene's peak and it barely happens.
3. **After Daren leaves, the chapter ends with a recap list of questions** ("Had she protected the boys…? Would Rohan stand with her…? What would the archive find…?"). The rewrite plan (§2.8) marked these final paragraphs for deletion.
4. **Rohan's pledge** gets no reply from Elara. YA readers want the beat after the pledge.

## Suggested changes
- **High: open on motion.** Cut paragraphs 1–3 to one: she sees both lawful roads end at the same wall, and she stands up. Let the reasoning surface during the walk.
- **High: make the archivist encounter a real near-miss** (about 100 words). He stops. He asks what she's carrying. She answers with something dull and true ("Shift ledgers. I'm under review.") while her pulse does exactly what Sen taught her it would, and the door sensor is right there. He nods and goes. That's the scene readers will retell.
- **High: cut the question-list paragraph.** Keep one question, the one about Rohan, and let it sit unanswered.
- **Medium: give Elara a beat after the pledge.** She opens her mouth, he's already walking, she watches the scar catch the blue light. Keep the "blue steel" image.
- **Low: four minutes to spare** is good. Let the reader see the clock once on the ride up.

## Keep
The ending image (three boys at the mine gate and Elara at her own door, both thresholds at once) is lovely. Keep it short.
