# Ch6 Marked for Removal: YA review

**POV:** Daren · **Length:** ~4,150 words · **Dialogue:** ~6% · **Job:** the villain's origin; Meru; the surrender; "Wait."

## What already works for YA
- **Villain origin as humiliation.** A young man publicly shamed by an authority figure is very YA (think Snow, or Draco). It gives Daren a motive teen readers understand.
- **The voluntary surrender** (taking off his own bracelet before they can take it) is a strong, specific choice.
- **"You thought surrender was an exit." / "It was only the first thing I needed you to give me."** A chilling chapter-ender.

## Gaps
1. **About 1,800 words of architecture before the Grandmaster speaks:** the hall's founders panels, the seals, the Phoenix, the guards, the ceiling. Each piece is good; together they're a museum tour. A teen reader starts skimming around the panels.
2. **The ceiling-as-second-display paragraph** is long, and it explains its own metaphor.
3. **Daren has no inner voice before the fall.** We're told he's ambitious and precise, but we never hear him want something. The humiliation lands harder if we've seen what he's losing.
4. **The Tamil speech** works, but four long blocks plus four translations is a lot. (The language-check tracker item still stands.)

## Suggested changes
- **High: cut the hall walk by ~40%.** Keep:
  - the founders panels where the workers are only background texture (that's thematic);
  - the gold seal in the floor;
  - the PHOENIX;
  - "The Grandmaster awaits."

  Cut the circular sealed chambers and compress the guards' scan.
- **High: give Daren one want before he enters.** For example, a memory of his first posting, or the director's nod he's been waiting six years for. One paragraph that makes him *young and hungry*, so the shame has somewhere to land.
- **Medium: shorten the ceiling paragraph to two sentences.** Keep "small and distorted beneath the approaching fury."
- **Medium: cut the second of the four Tamil passages** (the "weakness" speech) to English only, after a line noting the device kept translating. Three is enough to set the texture.
- **Low: "The act was small enough to be mistaken for defeat. It was not."** This is the "Not X. Y." construction. Let the act speak.

## Keep
Daren arriving thirty minutes early and checking his jacket's fall: "Kubera treated disorder as a confession." That's character.
