## The ten changes that matter most for a YA reader

1. **Ravi's age (High).** Ch1–13 present four new monitors on their first day (and Ch13 calls Ravi "the young apprentice"). But Ch17 ("since you were seventeen"), Ch19 ("eleven years of service", "eleven years learning what terminals remember") and Ch24 ("the hook he had used for eleven years") make him about 28. That pushes the book toward adult. **Fix:** make it three years of service, so he's about 20. The lines to change:
   - Ch19, ~line 105: "perhaps four times in eleven years of service" → "three years";
   - Ch19, ~line 133: "eleven years learning what terminals remember" → "three years";
   - Ch24, line 13: "the hook he had used for eleven years" → "three years".

   "Eleven years of refusal" for the Annex can stay; it predates him, and he grew up inside it as a student.
2. **State the cohort's youth in Ch1.** All four monitors are around 19–21, fresh Institute graduates by design. One sentence (Ch1 note).
3. **Contractions in dialogue.** Book 1's dialogue uses contractions about **6× as often** as Book 2's (around 53 vs 9 per 1,000 dialogue words). Book 2's teens and young monitors sound like elders. Keep the formal register for Grandmasters, elders and Flitzberg (it characterizes them). Loosen everyone under 25, especially Gage, Ben, Camilla and Otto (Ch19, 21, 25), plus Elara and Rohan when alone.
4. **Exposition load.** These chapters are the skim risks:
   - Ch14: ~5,000 words of history, 7 paragraphs over 150 words;
   - Ch17: the attention mechanics, explained three times;
   - Ch18: speeches;
   - Ch20: policy mechanics;
   - Ch23: the Guard lore.

   The fix is the same each time: turn lectures into Q&A the young characters drive, and move Ch14's history into Ch21's briefing, where the teens ask the questions.
5. **The ground cast is offstage until Ch18.** That's a deliberate decision (epigraph device), but the YA hook of the series is the four teens. Small, allowed bridges: a perimeter-feed glimpse of a girl at the gates in Ch2, and Camilla's file in Ch22 (already strong). From Ch18 on, the teens should be **louder** (messier reunions, jokes, swearing by implication) to match Book 1's crew.
6. **The Elara–Rohan thread.** It's the book's warmest relationship (Ch5 pledge, Ch12 confession and blue eyes, Ch24 shoulder to shoulder bare-wristed). YA readers will read it as a slow burn whether you intend one or not. **Decide.** If yes, add one beat of awareness per shared chapter (Ch12, Ch24). If no, cut the eye color and keep the bond as comrades.
7. **Make Flitzberg strange again.** Book 1's Flitzberg wears dead men's faces, collects nested dolls, plays music and serves cookies from the floor. Book 2's is a sober strategist. Two or three uncanny details in Ch14, 19 and 21 reconnect him.
8. **Book 1 facts Book 2 contradicts** (all quick fixes):
   - Ch1: Gage **280 lb → 220 lb** (Book 1 says 220 three times).
   - Ch18: the volcano is in a "fifth room" → it's **inside SACRIFICE**. Book 1 also reveals it was **one room all along**.
   - Ch18/19: "the small one" = Ben, and also "the small boy who lowered ten stones" = Otto. Clarify.
   - Ch21: name Camilla's **left** arm (Book 1, Ch24).
   - Ch14/19: Flitzberg is "an old man," but Book 1 gives "smooth pale skin, full cheeks." Decide whether that's a mask.
9. **Book 1 threads Book 2 hasn't touched yet.** Decide which belong to Book 2's climax and which to Book 3:
   - **Gerald** (Badge 732): the mirror box never built, "some of us pay attention to other numbers", his Trinity-2 grid knowledge.
   - **The Lake Wall / water-hammer plan:** a target and a weapon but no trigger. Flitzberg's mission is "salvation" for Sector One, and PLAN rehearsed collapsing their own mine. This looks like the mission.
   - Andre Lockhart, Lou, Jung ("his model had not predicted them"), Icepick.
   - The **wrist injection** that "stayed in them" (Book 1, Ch21).
   - The **flute player** who led Camilla down.
   - The captors' "None of them walks back" and the Electro-Barrage order: did Patal Loka stage it?
   - Rita alone, and Gretchen at the gates (name her!).
   - Ben's compass, lost; Rosa's fate; Leroy.

## Voice patterns to watch across the book
- **"Not X. Y." and "It was not X. It was Y."** These still appear in most chapters (the rewrite plan banned them). They're the most recognizable AI-prose tell.
- **"For the first time"** appears dozens of times. Pick the five that matter.
- **"Eleven" is a tic:**
  - eleven years, eleven postings, eleven characters;
  - eleven minutes, eleven steps;
  - eleven small things, eleven doors, eleven words.

  Vary them, unless the repetition is meant to mean something, in which case make it mean it once, on purpose.
- **Paragraph-ending aphorisms** that explain the scene ("…was the design", "…that was the point"). Often the paragraph is stronger one sentence earlier.
- **Recaps at chapter openings** (Ch9, Ch11, Ch16 and Ch23 each restate the previous chapter's events). Book 1 readers and binge readers don't need them.

- **Ages and bodies:** Gage 17, 6'5", **220 lb**, knife-scar "map" on his brow, badge #4055, carries Thorton's wrench. Ben Parker 17: father Oscar, sister **Gretchen**, the brass compass (lost in SACRIFICE), vow of non-violence.
