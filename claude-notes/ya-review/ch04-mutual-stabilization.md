# Ch4 Mutual Stabilization: YA review

**POV:** Elara · **Length:** ~2,650 words · **Dialogue:** ~7% · **Job:** Day Three; the sabotage; the report; the Infinity Guard; 72 h

## What already works for YA
- **The naming fight:** Elara types MUTUAL STABILIZATION and the system rejects it. That's a teen-vs-adult-world moment in miniature: you can see what's true and the authority won't let you call it that.
- **The report's classification ladder** (INDIVIDUAL RECOVERY → PERMANENT CONTAINMENT) is chilling and easy to read.
- **The Gage-carries-Otto image at 72 h** pays off Book 1 from a new angle.

## Gaps
1. **The Daren sabotage beat** ("someone had pushed her console's sensitivity…", "Daren was watching… with a flat, innocent expression") is the chapter's best interpersonal tension, and it resolves in one line. Elara never confronts him, even with a look.
2. **The Rosa/Emily fragment** at ~60 h: the rewrite plan (§2.1) asked whether the tower's view changes what the event meant. It still doesn't quite. It's a blur of telemetry that returning readers saw clearly in Book 1.
3. **A long run of system capitals** (UNAUTHORIZED…, INTERPRETIVE…, PRELIMINARY…, AUTOMATED…, OPERATOR…, INFINITY…). The rewrite plan's cap was three per chapter. Here they flatten the tension instead of adding to it.
4. **The final paragraph** ("A label became a schedule. A schedule became a restriction…") was marked for deletion in the rewrite plan. It's still here.

## Suggested changes
- **High: one look, or one line, between Elara and Daren** at the calibration moment. YA readers need the rivalry to be personal and visible. For example: she meets his eyes across the grid; he raises an eyebrow, the picture of innocence, and goes back to his loss curves. Keep it to two sentences. This also makes Ch5's "You made the system lie for you" feel like part of a running feud.
- **High: trim the system capitals by about half.** Keep MUTUAL STABILIZATION (rejected), INTERPRETIVE DEVIATION DETECTED, and INFINITY GUARD RESPONSE AUTHORIZED. Fold the rest into prose.
- **Medium: the Rosa fragment.** Either cut it to two sentences, or find the angle: the system logs Rosa as the aggressor, and Elara knows from the telemetry that she was dragging someone *to* safety. A wrong label the reader can see is wrong.
- **Medium: Rohan ordering a perimeter rather than an extraction** is the first time he defies the machine. Give it one line of his voice.
- **Low: cut the last paragraph's three-step "label → schedule → restriction"** and end on the act: *"She opened the archive access panel."*

## Keep
"Ravi's cursor rested over the group-risk field. 'What are you going to call them?'" makes a good first crack in Ravi.
