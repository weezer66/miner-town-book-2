# Ch2 Intake: YA review

**POV:** Elara, then Ravi (night coda) · **Length:** ~2,100 words · **Dialogue:** ~11% · **Job:** the boys enter; first deviation flags; the room's fault lines

## What already works for YA
- **Gage splitting his pouch** as seen from the tower: RESOURCE DISTRIBUTION DEVIATION. Returning readers already know the warmth of that act, and the cold label is the series' whole argument in two words.
- **"The boy someone had fed off the books was the first to feed someone else."** A beautiful private line. It's the chapter's heart.
- The recovery-station drink orders (tea, black coffee, electrolytes, bitter coffee) are good character shorthand.

## Gaps
1. **The checklists** ("Identity confirmed. / Medical baseline recorded.…") appear twice in nine lines. Once is atmosphere; twice reads as padding.
2. **Ben's backstory paragraph** (Leroy, his sister, eleven months of knowledge transfer) is a dense info-block about a character we only see as a dot on a screen. It's important (it pays off in Ch21), but it arrives as a file read.
3. **The monitors' argument in the recovery station** is the chapter's best scene, and it's short. This is where YA readers decide whether they like these four.
4. **Camilla is absent.** The rewrite plan (§2.2) wanted her on screen by Ch2. A quick feed glimpse would plant her for Ch22's reveal: a girl at the gates who doesn't leave.

## Suggested changes
- **High: cut the second checklist** (Rohan's). Replace it with one image, e.g. *"Rohan's list was four lines long, and every line was a weapon already loaded."*
- **High: extend the recovery-station scene by about 150 words of dialogue.** Let them be young and sharp with each other: a jab about Elara's terrible coffee, Daren needling Rohan, Ravi being quietly funny. Banter is the fastest way to make a YA reader care about adults in uniforms.
- **Medium: add the gate glimpse.** On the wide feed, a perimeter camera catches a family cluster outside the gates. One figure, a girl of about fifteen, is still there when the shift ends. Elara files nothing. (Ch22 then pays it off: "the girl at the gates" was Camilla.) This keeps the epigraph-only rule for the ground cast intact: it's a surveillance image, not a scene.
- **Medium: cut Ben's file paragraph by a third.** Keep Leroy and the sister; trim the cross-referencing mechanics.
- **Low: "Trinity did not call it watching…"** The rewrite plan (§2.8) marked this for deletion as a recap paragraph. It's still here.

## Keep
Ravi's night coda ("an unreadable one was a variable") makes a good POV handoff and sets up his arc.
