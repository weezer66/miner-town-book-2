# Ch17 You Are Welcome: YA review

**POV:** Daren → Ravi → Daren · **Length:** ~6,200 words · **Dialogue:** ~14% · **Job:** the invisible commute; the blind room; bribe → threat → Annex carrot; the black king

## What already works for YA
- **The cat that sees him.** The single best invention in Act Two. It's eerie, specific, a little funny, and it sets up the rule that pays off in Ch22 and Ch24. YA readers will talk about the cat.
- **The lamps going out one by one** around Ravi, and then "YOUR MOVE," is a cinematic, scary set piece.
- **The three-stage temptation** (money refused, framing shown, dream offered) is a great villain recruitment, and "The lottery was told to fall to you" is a gut-punch.
- **The king left standing, "not yes, not no,"** is a perfect gesture.

## Gaps
1. **⚠ Ravi's age** is established here as 28-ish: "since you were seventeen… the queue it has been standing in". Combined with Ch19's "eleven years of service" (and Ch24), this contradicts the YA cohort. See `00-index.md`.
2. **Six paragraphs over 150 words.** The longest are:
   - the explanation of how terminals mediate attention (~350 words, then restated);
   - Ravi's case for attending (~400 words);
   - Daren's closing doctrine (~450 words: oath vs. lens).
3. **The bracelet mechanics are explained three times** (the room's logs, the maglev queries, the HQ colonnade).
4. **Daren's final paragraph explains what the reader just watched** (doubt as a lens, gratitude as a guard).

## Suggested changes
- **High: fix Ravi's age.** For example: he entered the analyst track at seventeen and has three years of service, so he's about 20. "The queue it has been standing in since you were seventeen" still works and still hurts. See the index for every line that needs changing.
- **High: cut the attention-delegation explanation to one paragraph,** placed on the maglev and anchored on the cat. Delete the HQ restatement ("He did not take part in the negotiation…").
- **High: halve Ravi's pre-meeting reasoning.** Keep "His standing was already hostage. It had been hostage since the clock flashed red" and "he wanted to know." Cut the inquiry-path walk-through.
- **Medium: halve Daren's closing paragraph.** Keep the Master's three-line maxim (gold/fear/pride) and "Doubt was a lens, and a lens could not be taken off." Cut the rest; Ch19 and Ch20 show the lens working.
- **Low: "Who are you?" "A signature."** is perfect. Consider ending the conservatory exchange there and cutting "Whom do you serve?", since the lamps return on the next line anyway.

## Keep
