1. **The medic's line** (around line 113): *"Tomorrow," the medic said. "Tonight you lie still, think about the question, and give me less trouble."*

2. **Flitzberg's deferral** (around line 275): *"Tomorrow. All of it, as I promised your brother."* → ***"Today. All of it, as I promised your brother."***
   - Use "Today", not "this afternoon": Chapter 6's briefing may run into the evening.
   - Keep "Flitzberg's voice was still scraped raw from last night" and "You will have terms. Bring them."
3. **"Then you will have better ones by morning"** (around line 279) → something like ***"Then you will have better ones by the time I send for you."*** Keep his formal register, with no contractions.
4. **The collar** (around line 247). *"He stood in the doorway with his collar buttoned to the throat, over the marks Gage's fingers had left"* contradicts Chapter 2 ("He left the marks where they could see them") and Chapter 6 ("he'd worn an open collar").
   - Change it to ***"his collar open over the marks Gage's fingers had left."*** He never hides what was done to him.
5. **The one-way glass** (same paragraph). *"He'd been watching through the glass, she could tell from where he stood"* is impossible, because the glass works one way from her side ("The dark glass worked one way; she'd checked").
   - Change it to something like ***"He'd been there a while, she could tell from where he stood, and he wasn't pretending otherwise."***
6. **Camilla contradicts herself.** Earlier she **hides** the arm's self-movement from the medic, wanting "to understand it before anyone else decided what it meant." Then, in the Flitzberg scene, she **volunteers** it to him ("It moved. Twice. Without me.").

   - Adjust her lines around it as needed, but **keep** the exchange that follows: *"Who else can switch it off?" / "I can." / "From where?" / "From anywhere in this place."* (Chapter 9 depends on it.)
   - Keep "Ben stopped breathing. She heard him stop."
   - **Gage and Ben are in the room.** Make sure they react plausibly to learning, in this moment, that the arm moved by itself.
