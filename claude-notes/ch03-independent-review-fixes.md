## The reviewer's structural fix, adapted to v2
The reviewer read ch1–3 only, so two of its premises don't apply to the restructured book:
- **"Ch3 duplicates Ch21."** In v2 there's no separate Ch21. v2 ch3 *is* the rewrite of original Ch21's opening, and Ch21's Flitzberg scene (the three conditions) is v2 ch6. Readers meet the reunion once.
- **"Move the confrontation with Flitzberg into Ch3."** Moving all three conditions would empty out ch6, which builds the mission on them.

**What I did instead,** to give ch3 the same benefits:
- **A new closing confrontation.** After the reunion, Flitzberg appears at the connecting door. Camilla: *"It moved. Twice. Without me."* He: *"It moved before you did. That is not the same as without you."* She: *"Who else can switch it off?"* He: ***"I can."*** / *"From where?"* / *"From anywhere in this place."*
  - This pays off the medic's "There's someone you can ask. You'll see him again."
  - It turns the deferred switch question into a real threat she has to live with.
  - It sets up her ch6 condition ("Nothing in this place stays closed to me").
  - He refuses the rest until tomorrow, and she pushes: *"I've got them now."* / *"Then you will have better ones by morning."*
- **The ending.** Breakfast and the handprints image stay as the close, because every reviewer loved it, but they now come *after* the threat. Under the breakfast: "Nobody said Otto's name. Nobody said Flitzberg's." The last line leaves the doubt open: *"…the other one lay on the blanket and closed, half a breath behind, every time she thought of Otto. / She hadn't told it to."*
