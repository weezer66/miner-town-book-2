# Chapter 25 "The Door Beneath": Review and Suggested Changes

*Reviewed 2026-10-18 against Ch1–24 and `feedback-and-future-items.md`. Line numbers refer to `manuscript/chapter-25-the-door-beneath.md` as committed in f61390f. Nothing in the manuscript has been changed. Every edit below is a suggestion.*

**Overall:** The Otto and Ravi sections work and stay consistent with Ch1–24. The Elara section has two problems that would contradict earlier chapters (§1.1, §1.2) and a few smaller slips. Fix those and the chapter holds. At 2,230 words it is the shortest chapter since Ch2. §3 suggests where added length would do the most work.

---

## 1. Must fix (contradicts earlier chapters)

### 1.1 The Ancient One knows where Patal Loka is (lines 113–125, 165–175)
Ch21 (Flitzberg on the Balance Review): *"They know they met me. They could not lead anyone to where."* Ch14: the Chief Architect "could be met once a year but never followed home… no facility on any map a gana controlled." So no Grandmaster can put Patal Loka on a paper map, and none can say "meet me at the ring."

Telling Elara the name at all also breaks Ch14's circle ("four sitting Grandmasters… each told exactly once"). It also undercuts the Dhammapala, who refuses Rohan the same knowledge twice (Ch16, Ch19).

**Suggested change:** Keep the scene, the map and the call, but have the Ancient One stop short of the name and the place. The map shows the four houses and a blank where the public maps go blank. She touches the blank and says only that there is something *above* the houses' quarrels that she can ask to convene.

> Elara looked at the map. Four houses marked the upper cities. Below them, where every public map Elara had ever read went quiet, the paper was quiet too: no line, no crest, only the blank.
>
> "What is there?"
>
> "Something I swore never to name, and I will keep the oath even now." The Ancient One rested one finger on the empty paper. "Once a year, the four of us are taken to sit before it. None of us knows where we go. That is how it was built. But it can be asked to sit out of season, and I am going to ask."

Then at line 165, replace "At the ring. Patal Loka." with:

> "Before the table. Out of season."
>
> The pause was longer this time. "That has not been asked in my lifetime."
>
> "Then you will remember the day I asked it."

This keeps the payoff (a meeting the Master agrees to "without escort") and gives Elara her place "in the shadows." Readers who know Ch14 and Ch21 will recognize the Balance Review even though no one names it. **Delete lines 117–125** (the explanation of Patal Loka) and the paragraph at 127 that depends on them.

> *Note:* Ch14 never gives Grandmasters a way to call an out-of-season Review, so this adds one rule. Ch21 already says the office chooses the place each year, so an emergency sitting doesn't break anything.

### 1.2 Calling the Master of the Reserve tips off the one man who must not know (lines 143–167)
Per the tracker (OG lineage, decided 2026-10-17), the Master wore the band before Daren and fastened it on him. If the Ancient One tells him "the Guard is active… I have a witness," she gives him time to clean up and puts the witness at risk. Her own line at 111 says *don't* let him learn too early.

You have two ways to fix it:

- **A. She calls him on purpose, as a test (recommended; small change).** She suspects Kubera's chair because Ch23 has Kubera keeping "the box only as a relic." She calls him to see what he does, knowing the Sākṣī wall will show whether the Guard stirs afterward. This matches Sarvajña's rule in Ch23 (*you will find it when it is afraid*): she is frightening the hand's master, not the hand. Add two lines after the call ends (after line 169):

  > "You have warned him," Elara said, before she could stop herself.
  >
  > "I have told him I am looking. Nothing more." The Ancient One did not look at her; she looked at the dark grey wall. "Kubera swore to us that the box was a relic. Tonight we will learn whether anything in this city moves because I said so."

  Also fix line 111, where "him" is ambiguous. It reads as if the warning is about the Master, and then she calls him two beats later. Change it to: *"If the hand learns that we have named it before we know what it did…"*

- **B. She goes around him.** She calls the Dhammapala and the Arthashastri, not Kubera. That is cleaner, but it costs you the Master's line "I will come without escort," which is a good line.

### 1.3 A terminal on the dead-room table (line 189)
Ch18: in the dead room, wrist terminals are "surrendered at the door into a sealed locker." Suggested change:

> He looked toward the locker by the door, where his terminal lay sealed with hers. He did not get up. Elara could not tell…

### 1.4 The paper map in the dead room (line 181)
"Rohan looked at the paper map between them": the map was on the Ancient One's table, beneath the archive. She would never let it leave the room, and Elara is bound not to share what it shows. Change to *"Rohan looked at her empty hands."* Or simply cut the clause.

### 1.5 Terminal summons (line 95)
The Ancient One summons people on paper, carried by hand (Ch11; Ch23 with Anandi as carrier). A terminal pulse would put the meeting on the record, and that record is exactly what she is protecting. Suggested change:

> Before she reached the stair, Anandi was standing in it, a folded paper in her hand and nothing at all in her face. Beneath the archive. Now.

## 2. Should fix (logic and small canon points)

### 2.1 Ravi takes a folio he was only allowed to inspect (lines 73–83)
Line 73 says the folio "could be inspected by its originating analyst **in person**." Line 77 then has him carry it out, and line 79 has the clerk ask when he'll return it. Make the permission match what he does:

> …the source folio could be released to its originating analyst for one night's custody, against his signature.

Also at line 67, "verification against the **fiber** record": in Ch17, fiber *is* the paper. The thing he's verifying against is the terminal record. Change to *"for verification against the terminal record."*

### 2.2 How much Ravi learns (line 75)
"…and found the part the records had never explained to him." This is allowed (the reader still doesn't see what the file says), but it moves Ravi from *wanting* to know to *knowing* a chapter or more early. Under Plan C, his choice between instrument and hand is decided by what he does with the paper, and that works best if he hasn't fully understood it yet. A softer option:

> …and found the line he had underlined in pencil eleven years ago, still underlined, still waiting for someone above him to say what it meant. He did not let his face change.

### 2.3 How does the Ancient One know they were in the corridor? (line 99)
"You saw the hand return." Elara and Rohan planned the corridor watch in the dead room, which no one else can hear. The Sākṣī would show the hand's look-away orders at that hour, but not that two bare-wristed people were watching. One line covers it: *"Anandi told me where you would be standing. The wall told me who walked past you."* Alternatively, Elara reports it herself through Anandi's note.

### 2.4 "Three rooms away" (line 35)
In Ch23, the three asked to be woken in "the room two levels down." Change to *"Two levels down…"*

### 2.5 Elara's only road to the name is a dream (lines 87–93, 105)
The Ancient One's line "Do not turn recognition into proof" works well. It would work even better with one dull paper fact to set beside the dream. **Optional insert** before she reaches the stair (about 120 words): she checks the tower's public guest register for the Sector 1 cycle. The door on the fourth level was assigned to the Kubera monitor for the cycle and never released. Now she has a name *and* a room, both lawful to read, and neither one proves anything. (Details in `chapter-25-beat-sheet.md` §C2.)

## 3. Optional additions (if you want the chapter longer)

1. **A Daren section** (about 1,000–1,400 words; beat sheet §C3). This pays off Rohan's Ch24 warning that "every sensor in that tower will ask who we are and get no answer." Daren reads the tower's overnight digest and finds two unidentified persons on his stair at his hour. Bare wrists remind him of Baju. He has to stay still on purpose, and "You are standing in the wrong place" comes back to him, possibly meaning something literal. It also gives the Ancient One's call in §1.2A a target: the reader watches the Master's side react.
2. **Otto's first real question** (one line, after line 57). For example: *"Did they hold?"*, meaning the stones. It shows he came back carrying the same burden he went down with.

## 4. Line polish (your rewrite-plan rules)

The constraint block in `Miner_Town_B2_REWRITE_PLAN.md` §4 bans a few constructions that appear here. Fix them if you're still holding drafts to that list:

- Line 51: Camilla speaks with contractions in Ch21 ("It's late," "I'll learn it first"), so "You are out now" reads stiff for her. Suggested: *"You're out now."*
