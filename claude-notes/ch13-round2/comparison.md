## The Chamber
- **The Dhammapala's two anomalies.** She names two, from Elara's request: the doubled transfer and OG. "Two could still be coincidence, and Ashoka does not move on coincidence."
- **Rohan's third.** "The alert calibration was pushed to its ceiling through the Drona monitor's console. CORRECTION went twitchy with it… The record shows the console. It does not show the hand, and no inquiry has found one." He doesn't name Daren.
- **Her price.** "Find me the name" of whoever closed the flag. Rohan: "I do not know it, Grandmaster, and I will not find it out for you." He gives his reason: Dhamma above the order, and his word to Elara.
- **Her verdict.** "If you had given me the name, I would have had a name, and no one I could trust with the rest of it." Then the four investigators, "answerable to you alone". Ch20 depends on that wording.
- **Vayun.** "Two nights in the review room, and the footage you pulled was not yours to pull. I know. So does someone else."
- **Deferrals.** Two are left, "went below" and "Why did you step forward?". Cut: "Expect to hear more", "By whom?", the safeguards exchange (nothing later depends on it), and the flattery.
