# Drafting brief: new Chapters 1 and 2 (Book 2, v2 structure)

Both the Opus and Fable drafts use this brief, word for word.

## Ground rules
- **Never edit anything in `manuscript/`.** Read it only.
- Write only the two output files named below.
- **Keep the author's voice.** The existing chapters are the voice reference: close third, past tense, measured, precise, image-led, with an occasional dry line.
  - **Elara:** see `manuscript/chapter-01-assigned-to-witness.md` and `chapter-05-the-altered-record.md`.
  - **Camilla:** see Book 1 (`claude-notes/manuscript-v2/_book1-text.txt`), especially the chapters in Camilla's point of view, 6, 10, 15 and 18–20. Camilla is 15: sharp, literal, machine-minded, wry. She thinks in mechanisms, exits and counts. She calls herself the Eyes and Gage the Engine.
- **Prose rules** (from the author's rewrite plan). Avoid:
  - "Not X. Y." and "It was not X. It was Y." constructions;
  - "For the first time", "For a moment", "In that moment";
  - paragraphs that end on an aphorism explaining what the paragraph just showed;
  - "as though" (use "as if" at most once per chapter);
  - restating what the reader has already seen;
  - more than three ALL-CAPS system messages per chapter.
- **Do:**
  - Show emotion through the body or action, never by naming it.
  - Vary sentence length.
  - Include at least one specific, slightly odd physical detail per scene.
- **Young characters (under 25) use contractions** in dialogue. Grandmasters, elders and Mr. Flitzberg stay formal; the contrast is deliberate.
- **No romance between Elara and Rohan.** They are comrades. No lingering on his looks.

## Story decisions that apply
- Book 2 braids two timelines. **THEN** is the Trinity tower, starting at the Initiation. **NOW** is the hidden ring beneath the city (its name, Patal Loka, is NOT said in these chapters), starting the moment Book 1 ends. Mark each chapter under its epigraph with a one-line heading: `## Then: <place or day>` or `## Now: <place>`.
- **Ages:** the four monitors are 19–21 new graduates. Elara is 19. Gage, Ben and Otto are 17 and Camilla is 15.
- **Book 1 facts to honor:**
  - Gage is 6'5" and **220 lb**.
  - Camilla lost her **left** arm to a drone laser; the stump is sealed with blue sealant from a spray can.
  - Otto held ten stones open over the illusion's lava and let them close over himself. Now his body stands, breathing, eyes open and glassed over, mind gone.
  - Ben gave up his father's brass compass to the lava.
  - It was one room all along; one quarter of the crystal is still pulsing blue.
  - Mr. Flitzberg has smooth pale skin and full cheeks and a measured, manufactured calm. He wears other people's faces (he wore Pappy's and Gerald's in Book 1). He collects nested dolls.
  - Book 1's final lines: Gage grabs him by the throat, "Why? Otto is here and still gone. Camilla is half-dead. What did we ever do to you?" Flitzberg: "The question is not what I did to you. The question is: what will you do to regain the freedom that was once your birthright?" Ben: "What does that mean?" Flitzberg: "You are ready now. Ready for the journey ahead."

## Chapter 2: "The Throat" (NOW · Camilla)
- **Output:** `02-now-the-throat.md` (Opus) or `fable-test/02-now-the-throat.md` (Fable).
- **Source:** the first section of `manuscript/chapter-19-the-journey-ahead.md` (everything before the first `---`), retold from **Camilla's point of view**. Use its epigraph (verbatim from Book 1 Ch25).
- **Target:** about 2,800–3,200 words.

**Shape:**
1. **Camilla surfaces** half-conscious on the cold metal floor of the SACRIFICE room. Fresh sealant is cold on her stump; Ben's hands were there a moment ago. Her phantom left arm is still "there." Before her eyes open she hears her brother choking a man.
2. **She pieces the room together** the way she reads a machine: Gage's grip, Flitzberg not flinching, Ben on the floor, **Otto standing, breathing, eyes glassed**, the crystal with one blue quarter. She checks Flitzberg's jaw for a seam: whose face is he wearing?
3. **Keep Flitzberg's lines from Ch19, close to verbatim:**
   - "You are angry at the right person. Do not let that comfort you…"
   - "You were not punished. You were chosen."
   - "I do not know." (about Otto)
   - "No. It is not. It is only true."
   - "Some who fail die… That is the finished sentence."
   - "Yes." (about the arm)
   - "There was a time when the people of this region moved where they chose…"
   - Ben's "There's no counter in Miner Town where you can stand in line and ask for your freedom back."
   - "They have waited almost a century. They can wait one more night."

   You may trim. Don't reveal anything beyond what Ch19's first section reveals.
4. **Camilla is the one** who tells Gage to let go, or her voice is part of why he does. That's the Engine/Eyes dynamic: "If I tell you to stop, you stop" (Book 1). Make it physical and small: she can barely speak.
5. **The medics.** The oldest medic sets her case on the floor and opens it so Ben can see. Camilla, the machine-reader, reads the instruments. She'll want to know how the sealant works.
6. **Where Camilla is during the talk.** In Ch19 she's carried out early. Solve it so she stays in the room for the key exchange: she refuses to be moved until Otto is looked at, or the medics treat her on the floor first. She's carried out at the end. **Last image:** her count of the faces ("Four faces. Three looking back" is Book 1's line; echo it from her side) and the lights waking down the corridor ahead of the stretcher.
7. **Give Camilla one wry beat,** in Book 1's voice.

**Don't include:** the Ravi or Rohan sections of Ch19. The name Patal Loka. Explanations of the facility beyond what Flitzberg says.
