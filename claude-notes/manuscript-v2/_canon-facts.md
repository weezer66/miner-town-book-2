# Canon facts sheet (Book 1, Book 2, and the v2 decisions)

A compact substitute for rereading everything. If something here conflicts with a source chapter, **this sheet wins**, because it carries the newer decisions. Full detail is in `../structure-v2-two-crews.md` and `../book2-continuity-sheet.md`.

## Structure (v2)
- **Two braided threads:**
  - **THEN:** the Trinity tower, from the Initiation to Baju's death, about six weeks, led by Elara.
  - **NOW:** the ring beneath the city, from the night Book 1 ends to Otto's first finger movement, about two weeks, led by Camilla.

  They converge at "Maintenance Fault," then run as one timeline.
- **Two crews:**
  - **Below:** Camilla leads. Gage is her Engine and the Architect of the mission (he plans, she calls it). Ben is the heart and the pattern-reader. Otto is the stillness.
  - **Above:** Elara leads. Rohan is her Engine, the one she has to keep alive. Daren is her thorn. Ravi is the swing piece.
- **The name "Patal Loka" is not said in NOW chapters until the brief allows it.** The teens call it "the ring" or "this place." Flitzberg says "this office" and "this ring."

  - Lost her **left arm** to a drone's laser in PLAN, tied the tourniquet with her teeth, and said "Lost an arm, not the rest of me. Get back to the panel."
  - Blue sealant from a spray can seals the stump.
  - From NOW ch. 3 she has a **metal left arm** that lags "half a breath," listens to her shoulder nerves, and is much stronger than she is.
- **Ben Parker:** 17, 5'8". Non-violent, a pattern-reader, fixes radios and coils. Father Oscar (empathy as resistance; dead, inferred). Sister **Gretchen** waits at the gates. Dropped his father's brass **compass** into the illusion's lava.

## The mission (revealed to the teens and the reader in ch. 6; don't mention it before ch. 6)
- **The plan:** finish Gage's Book 1 plan. A **water hammer**: slam the turbine-feed valves shut so the water kicks back into the already-cracked junction in the **Lake Wall** (Basin Sector). That floods the Lower Hub, the turbine floor, the Sector-1 silver veins and the Trinity transport pods.
- **The window:** the ten-minute **Sector-Shift Change**, when the Lower Hub is clear of miners. The Junkyard, on the granite North-East Shelf 100 ft higher, stays dry.
- **Why:** it stops the push toward deadly ground in the silver near the lake, and it breaks Trinity's "straw" without an uprising Trinity can bill to the neighbors.
- **Patal Loka's part:** it supplies the **trigger**. The valves answer to Trinity's safety systems, and the ring can make them obey.
- **Open cost:** someone may still have to be in the tunnel the water claims first.
- **The date:** "nineteen nights" from the briefing. The same night, it will turn out, as Daren's flood of Ashoka's doors.
- **Roles:** Gage is the Architect and plans it (splinter models). Camilla calls it.

## Shu Chan seed (planted in ch. 12 and ch. 13; paid off later)

## Camilla's arm (D15, D16; author decisions 2026-10-19)
- **Where the core comes from.** The arm's listening core, the web, is a copy of the reader **Drona built for the Obsidian Guard**.
  - The ring got it through the standing consent. Drona's books show only Drona buying for itself, so the Ancient One doesn't know the ring took it.
  - It is "as old as this ring's charter," not older.
- **Who knows.**
  - **Flitzberg knows** where the core came from. If Camilla asks him directly, he must answer under the pact. He never deflects.
  - The engineer doesn't know ("I was not told where").
- **The hidden channel.** The copy kept Drona's **witness channel**: every command the arm gives is mirrored into the Sākṣī. Neither Flitzberg nor the engineer knows this.
- **What Elara sees.**
  - On the grey wall, a **second signature**: no look-away orders, only small joint commands (open, close, half a breath late, the gap shrinking), someone learning a hand. The location field is blank.
  - First glimpse: v2 ch20 "The Proof". It recurs at least once before First Contact.
  - The Ancient One can infer that the ring has the reader, but can't tell Elara why.
  - Elara can't cite the wall. The signature drives her questions, never her proof.
  - Camilla doesn't learn her arm is watched until the leads meet.
- **Payoff.** The arm (half a breath late, learns its wearer) and the band (a heartbeat early, on fear) are two halves of one invention. Camilla is the one who can read the band at the climax.
