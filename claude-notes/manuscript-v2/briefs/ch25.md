## Beats, in Otto's POV throughout
1. **Up, about 500 words.**
   - Otto's body is still learning its own weight: stairs, a lift, the city above for the first time. He's a Miner Town boy who has never seen it; one strong image only.
   - Rohan meets him. **Dry Otto:** he sizes Rohan up as a fighter, and Rohan sizes him up and finds nothing to size. "You're very still," Rohan says. Otto: "I've been practicing."
2. **The empty dojo at night, about 700 words.**
   - The Little Dragons' mat, the class suspended. One small trace of the children: Claudia's red tape, or a small sock on the shoe rack inside out (ch13).
   - **Vayun is already there,** on a cushion, drinking tea, his belt worn back to white. He doesn't stand.
   - He looks at Otto the way the old man in the shed did. **He doesn't ask Otto to perform.** He asks him to stand in horse stance, and watches.
   - **The recognition:** something in how Otto holds it. The toe? (Shu Chan's lifted big toe in ch12 and ch20, "like something under the floor was pulling at it.") **Otto's own toe lifts.** Vayun sees it, sets down his cup, and **his hand isn't steady.**
3. **Kalinga, about 1,000 words.** This is the heart of the chapter.
   - Otto says the word. He hasn't meant to. It's the eleventh stone (ch12).
   - **Vayun tells the history in short, plain lines, never as a lecture.**
     - After the six days, Ashoka kept a small post at the boundary to watch the sealed place below, "and they named it for the war that made our founder lay the sword flat."
     - Kalinga. **The post, not the war.**
     - Years later the post was dissolved and taken off the maps. Its last guard was **his student**, who "went below and never came back." He stayed below. He waited.
   - Vayun **knew him as Shu Chan**, or by another name. Your call; keep it simple.
   - **The breath:** Vayun recognizes it and **never explains it.** "He gave it to you." The blue at Otto's skin can show faintly if you want. **No butterfly** (keep it for the climax), or at most the memory of one.
   - Rohan stands at the edge of the mat and understands what he's watching: **an Ashoka line Ashoka thought had ended.** One line from him.
4. **The step after fight, about 700 words.**
   - Otto tells Vayun what Chan told him in the blue: it is time to fight. He fought, and came back.
   - **Vayun gives him the next step,** in the same voice as his tests in ch13: "Force that arrives without anger. **And the stop after the correct action.**"
     - The fist is the last thing.
     - After the fight comes **the stop**: when the threat has ended, you stop.
     - **"A man who is not afraid gives the other man nothing to read."** This is the seed for D4, Otto against the band, which fires on fear. Don't name Daren or the band.
   - **Physical teaching, kept short:** Vayun stands and tests Otto with one strike that stops a breath from his face, as the Grandmaster's did in ch13. **Otto doesn't flinch.** Vayun nods.
   - "Your teacher would have liked to see that." Or let Vayun say nothing and return to his tea, as he did in ch13.
5. **Down, about 100 words.** Otto on the way home, the four nights, Gage waiting at the bottom of a stair with his hands open. **End small.**
