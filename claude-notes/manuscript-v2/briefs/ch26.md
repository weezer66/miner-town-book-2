## Beats
1. **The digest, about 600 words.**
   - The unlisted room, under the dark display he never switched on. The tower's overnight digest: *two persons, unidentified, fourth-level service stair*, at his hour, on his stair.
   - **Fear arrives, and he holds still on purpose** (the Meru practice): the hand unclenched one finger at a time, the band cooling.
   - He takes **Baju's notebook** out from where he keeps it. **He hasn't burned it.** He doesn't know why. He reads the circles thickening toward his own door.
   - Bare wrists: someone else saw him the way the man in the corridor did.
2. **The Master, rings off, about 1,300 words.** This is the heart of the chapter.
   - Summoned to the summit, or he goes before he's summoned. The rings lie in their row.
   - **The Master already knows** Drona is looking (the Ancient One's call) and that the table will sit out of season. He has agreed to come without escort, and he's calm.
   - **His instruction is chilling but at story level:** before the table sits, the stair will be clean, and **the notebook will be ash.** He doesn't say *kill*; he doesn't need to. "Put down whatever you are carrying" now has an object.
   - Daren tells him the truth, for once: the band fires on fear. **The man in the corridor.** He wasn't chosen; his hand was afraid.
   - **The Master isn't surprised. He's too quick:** "I know what it does."
   - **The revelation, shown rather than confessed:**
     - Daren sees the Master's bare right wrist, rings off, and on it **an old pale ridge where a clasp once sat**, the same mark Daren's gold left (ch14).
     - Or the Master says, idly, "It is cold the first night," and Daren hears that he said it before, and understands **how he knew.**
     - Use either or both. Then **one admission, short:** "I stood where you are standing. Once." That's the double meaning of ch21's "You are standing in the wrong place."
   - **A hint for the climax, not explained:** the Master once walked, *wearing it*, somewhere no one is fetched to. "I know the way to the table. I did not need to be taken." Keep it to one line, no more.
   - **What the Master did with it is not revealed here,** beyond this: he wore it, he knew it fires on fear, and **he gave it to Daren anyway.** "Second only to mine" turns into what it was: a tool handed down.
   - **Optional:** why the Master took it off. The ch5 rule says it closes only on a hand the city has let go of, so he had to be stricken once, then restored. One line at most; or leave it a question Daren can't ask.
   - Daren keeps his face and says the right words. **One-third of the truth again**, but now it's Daren lying *to* the Master. "Second only to yours."
3. **The maglev, the cat, about 600 words.**
   - Down through the dark between platforms. A family, a mesh carrier. **The cat again** (the same family, or another).
   - **It sees him, and this time he looks back.** He doesn't file it. He sees what it sees: a man with something on his wrist he can't put down.
   - The band warms. He holds still, and it cools.
4. **He chooses, about 500 words.**
   - The unlisted room. The notebook in his hands.
   - **He doesn't burn it.** He puts it inside his coat, against his chest, the way Baju kept it, and the way Rohan carried Baju's notice. **Don't say that comparison**; the reader has it.
   - **He doesn't obey the Master** about the stair, the two unnamed persons. That's the choice: **he does nothing with the band, on purpose.**
   - **One line of the four-note hum.** He lets it finish, or he stops it, your call.
   - **He tries the band once more, and his fingers stop, as in ch19. That's still true.** But this time he notices why: there's no one he'd be without it. Then a small new thought: **maybe there is**. End there, not resolved.
   - **Optionally,** he thinks of the man's name, which he doesn't know and never will, and decides that someone should.
