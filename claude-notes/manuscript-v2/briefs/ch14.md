## Structure
1. **Meru, then the gift: Daren, about 800 words.**
   - Open on Meru at dusk. The terraces, the attendants who ride up each morning and aren't allowed to sleep there, and "They called themselves stewards." Keep it to about 120 words.
   - Daren is on Meru but not of it: Meru had made him and would not house him.
   - **The fearlessness test (YA review):** he passes a Kubera guard who watched his removal, holds the man's eye until the guard looks away, and feels nothing. Fear had lived in what Kubera could take, and all of that was gone. What he feels instead is hunger.
   - The library gift, in one tight run:
     - the ledger header, verbatim;
     - "Weaken with abundance";
     - "Drona called itself the conscience of the city…";
     - the doubled sum;
     - the Continuity Reserve;
     - the two letters, and the one line "Obsidian Guard."

     **State each point once** (the review flags three paragraphs saying the same thing).
   - Then Chanakya: "Kubera moved resources, but Chanakya moved the story around them," and Ravi's name.
2. **The commute: Daren, about 800 words.**
   - Dressing in the unlisted room in the Drona monitor tower. The band reads the small motor impulses that run ahead of every movement: "He put it on the way one wakes a hand."
   - The maglev, with **ONE paragraph** on attention delegated to the terminals ("People saw what their instruments underlined").
   - Then **the cat**. Keep it in full. It's the best invention in Act Two, and it pays off later.
   - Chanakya's lamp-lit platform: about 100 words, keeping "returning them annotated".
   - Cut the headquarters restatement of the band's mechanics.
   - Keep "Elara had traveled as a signature on a ledger. Daren convened as an absence on a calendar."
3. **The conservatory: Ravi, about 1,900 words.** This is the heart of the chapter.
   - Ravi's reasons for coming, **halved**. Keep "His standing was already hostage. It had been hostage since the clock flashed red" and "he wanted to know." He has his terminal on passive record, against the rules.
   - The lamps go out one by one. His terminal dies. The thick silence. YOUR MOVE.
   - He replays the losing line twice. "The game was never decided on the board. It was decided by whoever could reach the board from outside it." He realizes it's Chanakya's own teaching method.
   - "The lottery was told to fall to you."
   - **The three offers:**
     - the GRANTED ledger, refused with "a leash with the collar delivered first";
     - the routing trail with his own credential, "It is not true. It is only recorded.", and "You would be believed.";
     - the Analysts' Annex, with the memo that carries his name.
   - "Who are you?" / "A signature." **End the exchange there.** Cut "Whom do you serve?" The lamps return.
   - The black king set standing on the empty table: not yes, not no. Present.
4. **The gallery: Daren, about 450 words.**
   - Daren watching from above. Twice a cleaning drone maps the gallery as empty.
   - The Master's maxim, three lines: gold buys the poor, fear rents the weak, a proud man is purchased with his own future.
   - "Doubt was a lens, and a lens could not be taken off."
   - **New:** Daren turns the same arithmetic on his maker. The Master's power is ranked and recorded; his own isn't. "A recorded power had a ceiling. An unrecorded one had only appetite." And: **no one can revoke what he cannot name.** To cancel the two letters, the Master would have to confess them first. This should land as Daren's private confidence. The reader should feel the danger in it. **Don't hint that the Master wore the band himself** (guardrail).
   - He leaves the king where it stands. He rides home to the room no record says is occupied.
   - **One beat of the four-note hum,** in the carriage or on the walk.

## Rules
- **Ravi is 20, three years in the analyst track.**
  - "Since you were seventeen" is right: he entered the track at 17.
  - The Annex's "eleven years of refusal" stays, because it predates him.
  - **Never "eleven years of service."**
- **Formal registers:**
  - **the room's voice:** formal, no contractions;
  - **Ravi:** contractions in his own thought; precise, mostly formal Chanakya speech when he's answering the voice;
  - **Daren:** contractions in his thought.
- The band is on Daren's **RIGHT** wrist. Terminals are on the left.
- **System capitals:** the ledger header, YOUR MOVE, and GRANTED ×3 count as the three allowed. Fold the DEFERRED/UNDER REVIEW/DENIED line into lowercase prose.
- **Cut** the paragraphs that explain what the reader just watched (the review lists them).
- No romance. Elara appears only as a thought.
