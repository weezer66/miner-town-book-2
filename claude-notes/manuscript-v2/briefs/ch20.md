## Structure
1. **The dead room: Elara, about 900 words.**
   - Rohan tells her standing, to the wall: "Baju is dead." He keeps "it" even now ("a rule you drop when it hurts was never a rule").
   - **The review's grief beat (required):** Rohan says, roughly, "He had a counter. He timed everything. He'd have known to the second how long it took me to read the notice."
   - Elara doesn't say she's sorry. She asks what the notebook held. Baju's method was right: look for the erasing, not the thing.
   - "Then we are not going to do it again." The Sākṣī, whole, needs the Ancient One. "And you are coming with me." The Ashoka word must be given face to face.
   - Rohan pinches out the true flame. **"Lead."**
2. **The chamber: Elara, about 1,900 words.** This is the heart of the chapter.
   - The three-line note carried by Anandi; the answer comes in an hour. Present are the Ancient One, Sarvajña and Anandi at a lit console. Anandi's blank face means she argued and was overruled.
   - Rohan tells Baju's death without names. The terms: "You will use what I give you. You will never cite it. Anything you find by it, you will find again by some other road." Rohan's yes uses the Dhammapala's safeguards line.
   - **The Guard's origin, as a Q&A that Elara drives** (the review's main ask). She asks short, sharp questions ("Who built it?" "Why seal it?" "Then why does the archive still work?"), and the elders answer in **two or three sentences each.** Cover:
     - after the six days, Kubera's commission;
     - Drona built the reader (it reads the forearm nerves a heartbeat early; "It does not read a mind. It reads a hand");
     - OG;
     - one generation;
     - sealed in a box of four parts;
     - Drona swore it had destroyed the plans.
   - **"Drona lied."** Two words, Sarvajña. **Nothing after it softens it.**
   - The larger lie: every Guard command was mirrored into the Sākṣī by a channel the Guard didn't know existed. "It has been listening to silence for a century." / "It is not listening to silence anymore."
   - **The live wall:** plain grey columns. Look away. Record nothing. The oldest entries inside Kubera, "someone practicing", then the Drona tower, the room, the conservatory at nine, and **the line test in the fourth-level corridor.** Rohan: **"It is not a blank. It never was. It is a signature."**
   - Why it must stay secret: Drona has watched the other three for a century, which makes it "a tyranny that has not yet been noticed."
   - **Sarvajña, about 100 words:** "It is only as calm as the hand that wears it… the band acts on fear as readily as on intent… You will find it when it is afraid."
   - **The live query, Meru:** two household panels, "look away", minutes old. "The hand is up there now."
   - **D16, the second signature (required, new).** While the query runs, a second column begins filling that nobody asked for:
     - not "look away" orders;
     - small commands to an instrument's *own joints*: open, close, open, half a breath late, the gap shrinking each time;
     - someone **learning a hand**;
     - the location field **blank**: no house, no floor.

     **Elara notices before the elders do, and counts it.** The Ancient One sees it a breath later, and something in her face closes. **She doesn't explain it**, because she can't tell Elara why. **Elara can't cite the wall,** so the signature becomes a question in her and is never proof. Keep it to about 200 words. Don't name the arm or Camilla. The reader may guess.
3. **The Meru stillness: Daren, about 900 words** (cut the source's version by about 30%).
   - A public gallery above the lifted-water gardens; the household panels. His hand trembles, and **the band is listening to the tremor.**
   - **The boot**, the one detail he can't stop returning to.
   - "Nothing at all, on purpose." He unclenches his hand one finger at a time. He keys each step himself, slowly, and the first panel takes a quarter of an hour.
   - **Keep:** "including the things his hand told it that he never heard."
   - **He believes he can't take the band off,** because there's nothing underneath it.
   - **Dramatic irony:** two lines appear on a grey wall beneath Drona's archive, with his hour on them, and someone reads them.
   - Cut the repeated guilt ("numbers that were people" *or* "the absence of anyone able to tell him no"). **Keep one, not both.**
4. **The blue: Otto, about 1,100 words** (use the peer's notes in `_notes-for-later.md`).
   - **Start at the butterfly** (source line 217). **Don't repeat lines 207–215**, which ch. 12 used.
   - The yard behind the shed. Master Chan, the butterfly on his shoulder.
   - **Cut Chan's diagnosis by about 40%.** Keep:
     - "the fist is the last thing, not the first" (as an echo, because ch. 12 used it);
     - "You have adapted to yourself so completely that you have disappeared into it";

   - **Otto uses contractions.**
   - **Optional nod to Book 1** (the ch. 11 epigraph): Chan once healed his own locked hand by opening it in his mind, an hour at a time.

   - **Before the finger, the memories:**
     - Ben's hands on the glass;

5. **Coda: the medic, about 200 words** (include it in part 4, after a `---`).
   - One of the lines on the panel does something it has **done only twice before** (ch. 3 and ch. 15). This is a third, bigger event.

   - She marks the time and the phase and keys the alert. **She glances at the three names on the panel:** the people who asked to be woken.
   - "There was no one else awake to do it."

## Rules
- **Never technologize the elders or Otto.** The blue, the breath and the butterfly stay unexplained.
- **Only the band gets an engineering explanation,** and keep that short.
- **The Guardrail:** no hint that the Master wore the band. Sarvajña speaks of "whoever wore it", generically.
- **Ages:** Elara 19, Rohan 21, Daren 20, Otto 17.
- **Contractions:** the young use them; the elders and the medic don't.
- **"Half a breath":** the D16 signature may use it once. Otherwise avoid it (the ch3 peer note).
- **System capitals:** at most three. The wall's orders can be italic.
- No romance.
- Cut every paragraph that explains what the reader just watched.
