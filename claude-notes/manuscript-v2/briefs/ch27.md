## Beats
1. **The control room: Camilla, about 1,100 words.**
   - The ring's control room on the night, with screens and the people who work them.
   - **The listener:** her bench ear now hears rock, sometimes. She can't trust "sometimes," and she knows it.
   - **The crew splits:**
     - **Gage** goes up to the mine, to the Hub. He's the one who knows the run by hand, carrying the listener. He's the Architect.
     - **Ben** takes the stool in the screen room. He's the count, back from topside as promised.
     - **Otto** is three days out of a tank and won't go to the mine. He insists on staying near "the room where the table is," because something in him says that's where he's needed. Nobody can argue with the stillness.
   - **Camilla and Gage before he goes:**
     - few words, hands open, then not;
     - the split splinter;
     - **neither says who'll stand at the wall.** Both know.
   - **Flitzberg**, in his own face, on his way to sit with four Grandmasters. One line to Camilla that's true and costs him. For example: "Sooner than you are ready. I said so. I was right."
2. **Fetched: Elara, about 1,000 words.**
   - **The Ancient One takes Elara below blind,** as she said she would: "where you can see the work and no one can see you doing it." A closed car, a lift with no floors marked, no terminal (bare wrist again).
   - The four Grandmasters are fetched separately. Elara sees only her own. **The Dhammapala comes alone,** and Rohan doesn't.
   - **Rohan, turned back:** "without escort" cuts both ways. **One short beat in Elara's POV** before they part: he knows a way down (the lift in Drona's lower stacks that Ben came up by, ch24). He says he won't use it unless the night breaks. She doesn't believe him, and doesn't argue.
   - She's put in a side gallery above the table room, behind glass. **She sees the table:**
     - the four Grandmasters;
     - **Flitzberg** in his own face, which she has never seen. To her he's "a man with a soft face" who belongs to no house;
     - the Master of the Reserve, who arrives without escort, as he said.
   - **Don't name Patal Loka.** Elara doesn't know the word. She knows only she's below the boundary, and that this is the blank on the map.
3. **The route no one is fetched by: Daren, about 1,000 words.**
   - **He follows the Master's route,** the way "I did not need to be taken." He has kept the band on, and he uses it **only to open doors, nothing else, on purpose.**
   - **Doors that were never on any map** open before his hand rises. Old stone, then older.
   - **Baju's notebook** is against his chest. Someone ought to say the man's name in a room that's listening.
   - **The cat image returns for one beat:** in a dark lift wall he sees his own reflection and, for a second, what the cat saw.
   - He doesn't know yet what he's going to do. **He knows he's going to be in the room.**
   - Below, somewhere: **a boy standing very still in a corridor,** Otto, whom Daren has never seen. One sentence; Daren feels the band find nothing to read and doesn't understand it.
4. **Seated: Camilla, about 900 words.**
   - **Back in the control room.** On one screen the table room: the Grandmasters seated, the Master's rings off, laid in their row.
   - On another, the mine: Gage's lamp at the Hub mouth, and Ben's voice beginning the count.
   - **The ten minutes haven't started.** The first feed is still open; the wall is still dry.
   - **End** on her hand above the call, the metal one, waiting half a beat behind her, and the word not said yet. Something like: *Feeds.* Not yet.
