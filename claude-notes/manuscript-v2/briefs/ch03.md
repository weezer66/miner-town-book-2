## Shape

2. **The arm is offered.** Show the offer (in Ch21 it's only reported: "They offered. I said yes."). The medic, or an engineer the medic brings, shows her the metal arm:
   - dull grey, jointed at the shoulder, elbow and wrist, long fingers;
   - it listens to the shoulder, to what's left of the nerves;
   - it will learn her timing.

   **Camilla thinks of Gerald's leg** (Book 1 Ch14): the license that lapsed overnight, the system that "stopped recognizing him." Her first question is **who owns it, and can they switch it off?** The answer should be honest and only partial, setting up her later condition ("Nothing in this place stays closed to me," ch. 6). Then she says yes.
3. **The fitting** (keep it short, sensory, not gory): the first time it moves, "half a breath" behind her thought. One beat of fear, one beat of fascination. She's a machine-reader meeting a machine that reads her.
4. **Morning. She sends the medic to fetch the boys, and to take them to Otto first.** Ch21: "Your sister is awake… she told me to take you to Otto first. Her words." Camilla goes to the observation room herself, before them, or waits within sight of it. Show **Otto's tank** through her eyes:

   Camilla, being Camilla, wants to know how the cycle works. Ben's hands on the glass, if the boys arrive here.
5. **She's standing when Gage sees her.** Ch21: "Camilla was standing when they came in… Like the trial room, like the railing." Write it from her side. She *chooses* to be standing.
6. **The reunion.** Keep the strongest Ch21 beats, close to verbatim, from her side:
   - Gage crossing the room; his arms around her "on the side that was still hers";
   - **the metal hand rising a heartbeat late and settling flat between his shoulder blades**, so she feels it be gentle without deciding to;
   - "Lost an arm," she said into his shoulder. "Found one.";
   - Ben hanging back, and "You too. Come here.";
   - "Does it hurt?" / "No… It's late. Half a breath behind me…";
   - "They put that on you overnight." / "They offered. I said yes… What would you have done?";
   - she holds the metal hand out palm up, the way he once held a copper tag out to her (Book 1);
   - **the grip:** the metal fingers close a heartbeat late, then all at once, and he can't pull free. Ben laughs out loud for the first time since the mine;

   - "And it's learning you." / "Yes… I'll learn it first. I'm faster.";
   - end near "He believed her. That was what scared him." Here it's *her* side: what she sees in Gage's face when he believes her.
7. **Last beat:** the three of them, and the empty place where Otto should be. One small, specific image. No aphorism.
