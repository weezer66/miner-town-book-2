## Order (the review's main ask): Daren first, then Rohan, then Elara as the coda
1. **The corridor: Daren, about 1,700 words.** This is the heart of the chapter.
   - **His errand,** fixed for v2:
     - Kubera has already voted the review, and Ashoka has accepted it.
     - Daren is going up to Meru to see that the two councilmen who'll receive Ashoka's **ten-day confirmation of first postings** read it as their own good sense. A briefing note will arrive on their desks that their own staff won't remember writing.
     - Keep it to a sentence or two. **Don't use the source's "council sits the day after tomorrow."**
   - The service corridor on the fourth level, at half light, with pipes overhead and waist-high panels each showing a small green light. One cover is open.
   - A man crouches at the panel's log face: a technician's coat, a toolbag, **one palm flat on the steel frame.** **The reader should recognize Baju from ch. 16** (the climber's hands, the counter in his pocket, which clicks once) **before Daren learns anything.** Daren never learns his name.
   - **The bare wrist.** There's no terminal for the band to talk to, and his eyes find Daren and *stay*. Daren thinks of **the cat.**
   - The man glances from the log face to Daren and nods to himself. The screen says the corridor is empty while a man walks through it.
   - **The fear, and the band firing on it:** "his body wanted the man gone… the bracelet had never once waited for a flicker to become a fist." The band grows warm. **A line test** discharges through the frame while a man's hand is on the steel. Keep this at story level: a crack, a flash, the smell, a tool skittering away. **Don't add technical detail beyond the source.**
   - Daren's legs stop. "He had never been in a room when it happened. He had never heard it." He turns. One boot has come half off. The green light is steady.
   - "He had not opened that door. He had only been afraid in front of it." **Cut the source's mechanics restatement by half.**
   - **The fall beat, in full:** his left hand goes to his right wrist, hooks under the band and pulls. **His fingers stop.** He remembers his pale, bare wrist in the chamber with black doors, and what he'd be without the band. His fingers come out from under it. "That was the whole of the crisis. It did not last long."
   - **The notebook:** pencil, door cycles, lift holds, sensor gaps, circled. The circles thicken toward one room. Keep verbatim: "He could make every record forget him. He could not make the forgetting itself invisible."
   - He takes the notebook and hurries: whoever held the man's leash will find him. **One beat of the four-note hum,** wrong now: it comes up and he closes his mouth on it.
2. **The notice: Rohan, about 1,100 words.**
   - On the eastern transit platform, four hours into a guardian posting. Ashoka's rule: a death notice goes to the officer who signed the posting. **He arranged Baju's posting himself.**
   - "Maintenance fault." He reads it as Baju would have, and sees what isn't there: **no notebook** in the list of effects (toolbag, coat, counter). "Somebody had… taken the only part of him that remembered the floor."
   - "Paper keeps." He folds the notice against his chest, where Baju kept his.
   - **Grief, in the body, briefly:** the ration bar. Baju broke one and gave Elara the bigger half. One concrete memory, one line.
   - **The door warning:** he hears the Dhammapala in his head, "a door that is eager to break," and **doesn't stop.** Let the reader feel the risk.
   - **"Stop listening," made unambiguous** (the review flags it). He sends Kaan two words by a route no signal touches: *stop listening.* Then say plainly what it means: **the ear is to approach the analyst.** The slow way is over.
   - End the section: "Rohan meant to give it every reason to hurry."
3. **The household archive: Elara, about 1,000 words.** This is the quiet coda.
   - Lawfully, she's reading her own monitoring record of the boys. The gate scans stop. **Absconded. Slots released.** "Gage's father's number was already back in the pool." That's *her* 4055, and it stings.
   - The water test, reopened: the reagent strip drawn from his sleeve seam, the purple then grey, "Origin: unregistered… no action taken."
   - **The review asks for one human exchange.** A records clerk at the household archive, older, who knows the shack-rows (or Anandi, checking on her), with two or three lines. For example, the clerk says the Heights waste-fields are two days' walk "and nobody crosses them on purpose."
   - **The household record:**
     - Camilla, half-sister, two years younger, non-labor.
     - Flagged as a child for biting a guard during Gage's detention.
     - Flagged for two days' absence toward the Heights before the Initiation.
     - **Show that the strip is Heights salvage** (the "Logic Strip," made from waste), so the reader feels her cleverness as well as her courage.
   - **The Camilla mirror:** Elara at 15, in the freight pod, with a notebook on the Sanjeevani herb and three days on the ground while Teresa died. "Camilla had gone out to the Heights alone, the same way, for the same reason. The difference was that Camilla had come back with something that worked." Keep "Nothing forged. Nothing named. Only timing."
   - **Camilla's ration card** stopped being drawn the same morning. "Four people from three shacks, gone within a day of each other." Wherever the boys are, the sister is with them.
   - **End:** she closes the record and doesn't yet know about Baju. **One line of dramatic irony:** a notice is moving toward her through Rohan that she hasn't read yet. Or end on the girl's intake photograph, "eyes already busy with something out of frame."
