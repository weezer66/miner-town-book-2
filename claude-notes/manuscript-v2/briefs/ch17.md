## Structure

   - Daren asked for the audience before any summons could: a man who arrives first is presenting, not defending.
   - The rings lie in a row on the side table. Bare hands mean no seal and no record. It's the summit's own dead room, "though neither house knew it was imitating the other."
   - Keep:
     - "I taught the doctrine to a treasury officer. I am hearing it back from something else."
     - "Tell me what he is for. Not what he is."
     - **One** compressed answer: Chanakya will be asked to explain the pattern, so "We have bought the man who will be asked to explain us." Cut the routing-law paragraphs.
     - "How was he secured?" / "He refused the money. He was offered a future instead." **This is one-third of the truth, and Daren keeps the method for himself.** Make that beat land; the review says it's buried.
     - "Ashoka next… You weaken a blade by asking it to be a shield in every doorway at once."
     - "Second only to mine." / "Second only to yours."
   - **Guardrail:** no hint that the Master wore the band, though the bare hands may sit there for the reader to notice later.
2. **The Annex celebration: Ravi, about 800 words** (the review asks to trim the memo mechanics).
   - The archive director reads the decision aloud from a retrieval cart, the floor cheers, and hands keep finding his shoulder.
   - "The nine pages were the finest work that had ever carried his name. That was the problem. He had not written them." The memo argues the way he argues: it forged his mind.
   - One short paragraph on how the memo was logged from his console on evenings he spent otherwise, then carried to council by procedure "the way a river carries anything placed in the water upstream."
   - **"The city preferred its stories to have authors."**
   - The black king, standing.
   - **The slip of paper:** on paper, from memory, he writes a single file number for an old report he filed as duty required, which was sealed above his grade and never resolved. He folds it into his breast pocket behind the pens. **Don't say what the file is (Plan C).**
   - End: "He wanted to know."
   - **D9:**
     - The archive director is someone he has seen "perhaps four times in three years of service."
     - "He had spent three years learning what terminals remember."
     - **Never "eleven years" about Ravi.** The petition's eleven years stays.
3. **The mandate review: Daren, about 500 words** (cut the roster hack by more than half).
   - Ashoka's founding charter promises "a guardian within reach of every citizen," written for a city of a few hundred doorways. Now there are thousands, and the arithmetic is the weapon.
   - He needs Ashoka's own duty rolls. The band reads his forearm nerves a heartbeat early and carries the request out "looking like a knock from inside." The house seal flickers from Drona's to Ashoka's and back.
   - **Keep:** "whether he was using it, or whether it had simply learned the way he moved and was moving with him, a heartbeat ahead." This sets up ch. 19.
   - The review's language: "The guardians have never failed the city. The city has failed to pay for its guardians."
   - "He did not invent a single duty. He woke the ones Ashoka had already sworn."
   - He needs it closed against every rule, and he has an analyst who knows the rules.
4. **Room Four: Ravi, about 1,100 words.** This is the heart of the chapter.
   - A message after the night bell, with no sender: "Policy Review Room Four. Tomorrow, second hour after midday," and a string of **nine** characters (not eleven; the review flags number repetition).
   - The room is booked under his name, which he didn't book. The console "became a different screen while keeping the same face." *Work.* *Make it unrefusable.*
   - **Compress the four closures** (charter, funding deferral, council tabling, the Grandmaster's prerogative) to **one or two sentences each.** Spend the saved words on his body:
     - his pen hand cramping;
     - him checking the door glass when someone passes;
     - **one concrete image of the cost:** an Ashoka responder, perhaps one of the two from the maglev in ch. 14, pulled off a real emergency to stand at a ceremony.
   - **The gap, given a full physical beat.** He could fix the postings and officers from outside. That would be tighter, and obvious. "The gap was the price of the disguise." His fingers hover. "He left it open. He told himself it was craft. He was honest enough to notice it was also the only door in the document he could leave ajar."
   - He fixes **seven** small clerical faults (not eleven).
   - "Not what does this document say. Who needs it to say that?" The answer: whoever needs Ashoka elsewhere.
   - *Accepted.* "Good review?" / "Routine." His hand goes flat over the breast pocket and the slip.
5. **The flood: Rohan, about 700 words.**
   - Daren's two-line send-off: he reads it in totals and doesn't notice the gap. "The board was his. He did not think about the pieces." Put this at the top of part 5, or at the end of part 4 as a short Daren beat.
   - "The flood reached Ashoka on a gray morning, dressed as an honor." The duty board spills onto a second board by midday. **Rohan's name is on nine of the new postings.**
   - **New, required (the Little Dragons cancelled):** on the board's list of suspended training sessions is his Little Dragons class. He sees Claudia's name on the roster, with her new brown belt noted beside it. One short, stinging beat: he has to tell them, or a clerk already has.
   - His superior: "Paper keeps. Platforms do not." Rohan counts the gifts: money to Drona, an archive to Chanakya, wages to Ashoka. "Generosity, Elara had said. He feeds them. Now it was feeding him. And the first bite was his time."
   - Evening: the revised orders. The Dhammapala has taken the ceremonial stands herself and moved two rotations, so his nine postings become **four**. The Drona review stays.
   - **The strip of paper, verbatim:** "A flood is also a current. It shows you where the water wants you not to go. Carry your share, no more. The house will hold the weight. You hold the path." He burns it at a true flame.
   - **End:** "Wherever the flood pushed hardest — that was the direction to walk."
