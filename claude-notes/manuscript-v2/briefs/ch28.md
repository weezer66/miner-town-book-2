## Beats
1. **The ten minutes: Camilla, about 1,200 words.**
   - **Ben counts the men off the Hub.** It's the shift change, and **the Hub is empty of miners. Say so plainly.** The turbine man with the sick kid goes home on time tonight; Ben says so.
   - **"Clear."** **"Feeds."** **"Closed."** These are the rehearsal lines, real now.
   - **The listener is with Gage, and it only hears from inside the tunnel.** "Someone may still have to carry it in." **He carries it in.** That decision was always his, and it's the payoff of the finger on the split splinter. Camilla knew; it still lands.
   - **Gage's voice on the line:** "Landed. Again."
   - **Keep everything at story level.** The wall "answers." No valve mechanics.
2. **The ring breaks: Camilla's choice, about 900 words.** This is interleaved with part 1, or follows it.
   - On the table-room screen, **a door opens that nobody opened.** The ring's own sensors show it. Something walked in on the old route. The Master doesn't look round; he's been expecting it.
   - **Otto, on another screen, moves toward that door.**
   - Camilla has two fronts and one voice. She's the hinge.
   - **Elara comes into the control room** (the author's decision). She was brought down to watch and has walked out of her gallery. **This is the first time the two leads meet.**
     - Elara knows the signature of that door ("It opens before the hand rises").
     - **The blind spot:** she tells Camilla, truthfully, that **the city above isn't watching the wall tonight.** Ashoka is in every doorway, and Drona's wall is turned the other way. The watcher hands the watched the one thing she never had.
   - **D16, told:**
     - Elara looks at Camilla's metal arm and knows the column she counted: *open, close, a fist, no house, no floor.*
     - She tells her: **"Your arm talks to Drona's wall. It has since the day they fitted it."**
     - Camilla takes that in **in one beat, not a scene.** There's no time.
     - **Don't resolve it.** It matters in ch29, when she reads the band.
   - **The choice:** Camilla gives the last of the wall count to **Ben**: "You've got it. Call it if I'm not here." Then she goes for the ring. It's her climax, and it costs her the line to Gage.
3. **The tunnel: Gage, about 1,200 words.**
   - Gage in the dark run from the Hub to the junction: his left hand on the rock, the props counted (thirty-one, forty-one; use the earlier chapters' counts), the beam he ducks for, the drain sill, the listener against the rock.
   - **"Landed. Again."** He reports each blow to Ben now, because Camilla's voice has gone off the line.
   - **The wall goes,** at story level: sound, pressure, the rock talking before it breaks, the lamps going (Book 1). **Gage turns and runs the run backward by hand.**
   - **The cost (author decision): it works too well.**
     - The silver pipeline breaks, and so do the turbine floor and the pods.
     - **And something of Sector One's goes under too.** Recommended: the old pump house at the edge of the Lower Hub that draws Miner Town's ration water.
     - **Gage sees it go and can't stop it.**
   - **Gage is badly hurt and alive** (author decision): caught by the water or a collapse at the last prop. **End his section on the line going quiet.** His last word to Ben is a count, or "Through."
4. **Coda: Elara, about 400 words.**
   - Elara alone in the control room, with Ben on his stool, still counting into a silent line. **She doesn't know Gage.** She sits beside the boy and listens with him.
   - Or she stays to read the ring's screens while Camilla runs.
   - **End** on the table-room screen: Daren in the doorway, and the Master's rings in their row. **That's ch29.**

## Rules
- **Safety and tone:** this is a dam-break set piece, written like the ending of a disaster story, never like a manual. **No technical method** for the hammer, valves or flood. **No one innocent is in the Hub when it goes,** and the narrative must make that clear. The cost is property, water and Gage's body.
- **Ages:** Camilla 15, Gage and Ben 17, Elara 19.
- **Voices:** the young use contractions. Elara and Camilla meet as equals, and neither defers.
- **No romance.**
- **Don't reveal the Master's history or Ravi's file.**
- **"Half a breath":** at most once, for the arm.
- **System capitals:** none, or at most one.
- Keep "the way X" similes to two at most.
