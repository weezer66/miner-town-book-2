## What the chapter must do
1. **The workshop** (Camilla's condition, paid). Two levels down, third door, "waiting for someone like you for longer than you have been alive" (ch. 6).
   - It's real and lived-in but long unused: benches under cloth, tools hung on outlines painted on the wall.
   - **One odd detail:** a tool outline with no tool in it.
   - **A shelf of old technical manuals of the same printing and binding as Pappy's Master Scripts**, the ones under the floorboards at home (Book 1). That's how Pappy could have had them: they came from places like this, through the Junkyard. Camilla notices, and doesn't know what it means. **Plant it lightly; don't resolve it.**
2. **She opens her own arm.** With the engineer (the thin man with pencil stubs behind his ears, from ch. 3) watching but not touching, Camilla takes off the cover at the forearm.
   - Inside there are no wires. Under the casing is **a lattice finer than hair** lying against where her nerves would be, listening.
   - Near the shoulder cup she finds **a maker's stamp worn almost smooth**, an old design she can't read. The engineer says the listening core is "older than this ring's charter. We copied it. We did not invent it."
   - **Don't name Drona or the band.** This is a seed. The reader will later recognize the same lattice in Daren's obsidian band, and it's how Camilla will understand the band at the climax.
   - Show her competence and fascination. She puts it back together right.
3. **Flitzberg's strangeness.** Camilla goes to ask "the someone" (ch. 3: "There is someone you may ask") the switch question directly: who else can turn her arm off?
   - She finds him in his rooms: **nested painted dolls**, music playing, drawings on clean paper (Book 1).
   - His answer is honest and formal. The medic, the engineer and he can stop it, and no one above. "And when you have taken it apart to the last wire, you will know whether I lied."
   - Give him **one uncanny beat**. For example, he opens a nested doll to show the smaller one inside, or she asks about the faces he wore (Pappy's, Gerald's) and he answers in a way that is both chilling and sad (he keeps the faces of people this office owes).
   - **Don't explain the face technology.**
   - He's formal, she uses contractions, and she gets the last word.
4. **Gretchen at the gates.** The ring has feeds of Miner Town. Ben finds, or is shown, the gate feed, and **his sister Gretchen is standing at the gates**, as she does every day. He can't go to her or send word: no one comes back (ch. 6).
   - Ben's reaction is physical and quiet.
   - Camilla scans the same feed for **Rita** and doesn't find her, and doesn't know whether that's better.
   - **Keep it short and devastating. No speeches.**
5. **Ben's compass.** Ben's hand keeps going to the pocket where his father's brass compass used to be (lost to the lava, Book 1). Camilla notices.
   - **Ending:** in the workshop she magnetizes a needle (stroked on a magnet from the bench), floats it on a cork in a dish of water, and gives it to Ben. A compass that only knows one direction.
   - Ben's response: one line, or none.
   - **Last image:** the needle settling.
6. **Gage, briefly.** He's at a bench rebuilding the splinter model of the mine from ch. 6, the Engine-Architect. One exchange with Camilla about the trigger, "nineteen nights," and the question neither of them says aloud: who goes into the tunnel.
7. **Otto, briefly.** One mention: someone is sitting with the tank, or the flat line on the panel.
