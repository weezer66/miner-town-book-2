## Structure
1. **Otto wakes: Camilla's POV, about 1,400 words.** This is the heart of the chapter.
   - **The night after the finger.** The alert from "two levels down" wakes the three of them. Camilla, Gage and Ben run.
   - Through the glass, Otto's hand opens: the whole hand, slowly. The medic speaks through the tank's speaker. "Follow my finger. Palm to the glass." **His palm stays on the glass, and Gage puts both of his against it from the other side.**
   - The drain, by stages. The breathing line comes out. Medics move him to the bed beside the tank. Blanket. Eyes trying to make a world out of light.
   - **First words, required:**
     - Otto looks at Gage, and his lips move before any sound: **"You promised."** He means Gage told him to jump.
     - Then, dry, Book 1 Otto: **"I jumped. Took me a while."**
     - Gage's hands: let them do something they've never done in this book.
   - Otto's first real question (review §3.2): **"Did they hold?"** He means the stones. Ben answers.
   - Otto's eyes go to Camilla's **left** arm, the metal one. "I know. I'll tell you the rest when your head's ready."
   - **The crystal (required):** before or after the wake, someone (Ben, if he went for it, or a glance through a doorway) sees **the blue quarter change**. **Decision:** it **stops pulsing and holds steady**. It doesn't turn green. It's just still blue, and no longer cycling with the tank. Nobody can explain it. Keep it to one or two lines; don't explain it.
   - **The countdown:** **seven nights** left of the nineteen. Camilla counts. Otto doesn't know about the mission yet. She decides not to tell him tonight.
2. **The paper: Ravi, about 700 words.**
   - The records archive at first bell. He goes through the public gate with his terminal on, because the gate needs to know him. Inside, the terminal stays dark in his pocket.
   - The paper index; the sealed case; "I filed the report. I want the original folio." The original is released to its author for **one night's custody, against his signature in the paper ledger.** "Tomorrow." It isn't a promise he knows he can keep.
   - **Plan C:** he reads only the line he underlined in pencil in his first year, still underlined, still waiting for someone above him to say what it meant. **He doesn't learn what the file means.** Don't reveal its contents.
   - **The appointment** (Kaan's slip, "Room Four. Two hours."):
     - Two hours after first bell, he goes to Room Four.
     - It's empty. There's a binder on the chair with a spine mended in brown tape: **the porter's binder** (ch21).
     - Inside it is one retrieval tag, block letters: *Tomorrow. Bring what you took.*
     - He sits with the folio against his chest. **Kaan does not appear and isn't named.**
3. **The register and the call: Elara, about 1,300 words.**
   - **The guest register (§2.5):**
     - The tower's register desk, at opening. Paper, lawful under her code. It lists the borrowed rooms for the Initiation cycle.
     - **The fourth-level door was assigned to the Kubera monitor for the Initiation watch, and never released.** It's in a clerk's hand.
     - Now she has a name and a room, both lawful to read. Together they still aren't proof that he's the hand.
   - **Anandi** on the stair with a folded paper: *Beneath the archive. Now.*
   - **The chamber.** The grey wall is dark. A paper map lies on the table: the four houses, and a blank where every public map goes quiet.
     - The Ancient One: "Anandi told me where you would be standing. The wall told me who walked past you."
     - Elara gives her the register line, name and room, and keeps recognition separate from proof. **"Good. Do not turn recognition into proof."**
     - The Ancient One knows about the amber reader too: the tower's overnight digest logged **two unidentified persons** on that stair at that hour, and **the hand reads digests.** "It will know someone stood there." That's the sensors flagging the hunters.
   - **Out of season (§1.1):** "Something I swore never to name… Once a year, the four of us are taken to sit before it. None of us knows where we go… But it can be asked to sit out of season, and I am going to ask."
   - **The call (§1.2A):**
     - The old call plate: four shallow marks around a blank center. She turns Kubera's mark.
     - "The Guard is active. It was seen in Drona." / "You have proof?" / "A witness, and a pattern." / "Who?" / "Someone I trust."
     - "Before the table. Out of season." / "That has not been asked in my lifetime." / "Then you will remember the day I asked it." / **"I will come without escort."**
     - After the call: "You have warned him." / "I have told him I am looking. Nothing more… Tonight we will learn whether anything in this city moves because I said so."
     - **The guardrail:** she suspects only that Kubera kept "the box." **Nothing hints that the Master wore the band.**
   - **The dead room:** Elara tells Rohan the register line. Their terminals stay in the locker. He tells her the amber reader saw them, and **"It knows two people stood there."** The Dhammapala: "That's your decision." He doesn't get up. End on that, or on his hand flat on the face-down sheet.
