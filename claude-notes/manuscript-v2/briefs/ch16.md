## What the chapter must do
1. **Why Ashoka, briefly.** Open with Elara working out why they can't meet in Drona: "Because Drona is the crime scene." Rohan adds that an empty room between platforms would be worse, and that the safest place to stand is "inside your own lawful shadow." Keep it to about 200 words.
2. **The dead room as a lawful object:**
   - no lamps, sensors or network;
   - terminals surrendered into a locker at the door;
   - paper only;
   - the inner locker with one key-slot per person, cut fresh each session, opening only when every key turns together;
   - swept by hand by two officers who don't know each other.

   Let Elara feel her bare wrist: it echoes ch. 1's bare-wrist seed. One sentence, no explanation.
3. **The crew, introduced through action, not speeches** (this is the review's main ask). Each specialist is *doing something* when Rohan names them. Then Rohan gives **one or two lines each**, with the call sign and its meaning:
   - **Nayan, the eye:** already copying the wall onto paper, in pencil, dated. Show her habit of **keeping her own dated paper copies of everything she reads, so that when a record changes overnight she can prove it was ever different.**
     - **Seed (required):** she mentions, in passing, that she has dated copies of all four houses' public books, and **no one has ever asked her to lay them side by side.** Nobody picks it up. Don't explain it.
   - **Baju, the arm:** lean, weathered, a climber's hands. He's checking the door seal with his palm and has a step-counter in his pocket that clicks. "Floors cannot be told what to remember."
     - **The warm Baju beat (required).** He'll die at the midpoint (ch. 19), so the reader has to love him now. Give him one generous, human moment. Suggestion: he notices Elara hasn't eaten, breaks his ration bar in half and slides her the bigger piece without comment. (This echoes Gage's "first to feed someone else".) Or something equally small and warm: a dry joke at his own expense, or tea he brought on paper principle. **No backstory dump.**
   - **Kaan, the ear:** Elara didn't notice he was in the room until he's named. A cover posting starts tomorrow. He asks no questions; he receives.
   - **Jeebh, the tongue:** open-faced, a market trader's warmth. He cracks a joke to break the tension. He keeps the unit invisible "by keeping the city bored."
   - **Rules, kept short:** they don't know each other's true names or units. "No one can surrender what they were never given." Keep both "A body has one will. If it had two, it could be pulled apart at the joint." and "I am the only door in this body. And a door is the one part of the house that is built to take the breaking." Elara will not see these four again; everything goes through Rohan.
4. **The wall: three instances,** on handwritten panels:
   - the doubled transfer;
   - the two-letter signature;
   - the calibration pushed through Elara's console.

   "What makes these one thing and not three?" "Direction." The acts are designed to be found one at a time. Nayan: "Then the pattern is the evidence." Rohan: "The houses have never put their records in one room." Elara: "That is what this room is for."
5. **The Sākṣī tripwire,** about 150 words. Elara has been watching one band of witness mirrors: cross-house allocations. "Generosity had become her tripwire."
6. **The fourth instance arrives.**
   - A double knock. Nayan's runner has hand-copied the morning bulletin from the public terminal: Kubera's allocation council has approved the Analysts' Annex **in full**.
   - Elara knows the petition: it was refused every cycle for eleven years. That's the petition's history, which is fine.
   - The memo is "signed by an analyst named Ravi," "eleven years of refusal answered in nine pages."
   - Baju and Rohan test it from both sides. Elara: "It is perfect… This memorandum has no rings." **"Generosity is the fingerprint."**
   - **The pronoun correction:** Nayan says "He," and it isn't agreement. Rohan: "From tonight, inside this room, it is the hand. It."
7. **Rohan divides the hunt** in **four short exchanges,** one per specialist, each answering in a line (the review asks for this; no 600-word speech). Jeebh is funny: "Three sub-audits. I'll make them so dull I fall asleep filing them."
   - Kaan is told to watch Ravi without approaching him: an instrument picked up, or the hand signing itself as one.
   - "This is not history. It is traffic. It is still moving."
   - "Fourth instance. Fourth panel." **Baju is the one reaching to pin it up.**
8. **Rohan coda, about 500 words, Rohan's POV.** That night, with the Dhammapala, two cushions and one oil lamp: a true flame, nothing recording.
   - He reports the body's shape, not its faces.
   - "You have stopped saying he." "The mind hunts what the mouth names."
   - **Keep verbatim:** "A door that is willing to break serves the house. A door that is eager to break serves only its own story. Watch which one you are becoming. The first keeps people alive. The second is merely brave."
   - **Keep the instruction later chapters depend on:** "If your hunt ever crosses a path that feels like your own thinking arriving ahead of you — do not chase it. Mark the place, and bring it to me."
   - "Whose path would it be?" / "If the balance is kind, you will never need to know."
   - She puts out the lamp with two fingers.

## Rules
- **Ages:** Elara 19, Rohan 21. The specialists are older, but keep them unaged.
- **Register:**
  - Elara and Rohan use contractions in thought and in casual speech; Rohan goes formal when he's giving orders;
  - the Dhammapala is formal;
  - the specialists each have their own voice: Nayan clipped, Baju dry, Kaan almost silent, Jeebh chatty.
- **Pronunciations:** keep "(NAH-yahn)", "(BAH-joo)" and so on **only at first mention, if at all.** Better to drop them from the prose.
- **No Flitzberg section and no Daren section** in this chapter.
- No romance.
- **System capitals:** none needed. If the bulletin's title is in capitals, that counts as one.
