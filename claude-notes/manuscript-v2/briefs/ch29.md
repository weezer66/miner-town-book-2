## Beats
1. **The Master moves first, about 800 words.**
   - **The turn:** the Master speaks to the four, calmly, before anyone can accuse him. He names Daren as the Guard: Kubera's struck officer, who stole the relic from the sealed box and has worn it since. "I came without escort to bring him to you." **He means to make Daren the whole of it.**
   - Every eye goes to Daren. **Daren's fear rises,** and the band warms. The ring's instruments are in reach of it: lamps, doors, panels. Something in the room hums.
2. **Otto faces Daren (D4), about 900 words.**
   - **Otto walks between them, unhurried.** He stands in front of Daren at arm's length, hands open, **not afraid.** The band reaches for Otto and finds nothing to read (as in ch27). **It can't fire on what isn't there.**
   - **There's no fight.** Otto doesn't strike. "The stop after the correct action" means here that the correct action is to stand still. **One line from Otto, dry and short.**
     - Suggestion: "You can stop. Nobody here's going to hurt you." Or something about the stones.
   - The blue at Otto's skin may show faintly. **No butterfly; or, at most, Otto feels one.** Nothing explained.
3. **Camilla reads the band (D15), about 900 words.**
   - **Her arm hears the band.** Close up, her listening core feels the band's reach as a signal it knows, because they're two halves of one invention. Her arm runs half a breath late. The band runs a heartbeat early, and the gap between them is the size of a fear.
   - **She says what it's listening to,** aloud, in Camilla's exact, impatient way: "It's not you. It's a beat ahead of you. It's listening to you being scared, and you're scared of *him*." She looks at the Master.
   - She has known for an hour that her own arm is watched (ch28). **One beat:** she uses that knowledge here, at the worst possible moment, and it holds her steady instead.
4. **The bare wrist (D7), about 700 words.**
   - **Daren takes the band off himself.** His fingers hook under the edge, **and this time they don't stop.** There's someone underneath now: the man whose name he means to say.
   - The band comes off, cold. **The wrist:** pale, ridged, unfinished, bare.
   - He lays it on the table **beside the Master's rings.**
   - **Then the notebook:** he lays Baju's notebook on the table too. "A man died in the fourth-level corridor of the Drona tower, because my hand was afraid. He'd found my door. This is his. I don't know his name."
   - **Rohan answers from the doorway.** He has come down by the runner's lift.
     - **"Baju."**
     - That's the name, said aloud, in a room that listens.
5. **The door that is eager to break: Rohan and Elara, about 600 words.**
   - Rohan crosses the room toward Daren, Baju's commander toward the man who killed him. **Fury, held.** It's the "eager door."
   - **Elara is there** (she has come from the control room) and stands in his way, not touching him.
     - She keeps him alive and keeps him himself.
     - **She uses his own maxim:** "Stop when the threat has ended." Or Claudia's "falling isn't the end of the lesson."
     - **He stops.** He doesn't forgive. He doesn't strike.
6. **Exposure, about 400 words.** End the chapter on the stakes, not the verdict.
   - **Daren, bare-wristed:** "He wore it first," and he points at the Master's wrist. The ridge where the obsidian sits. **"It is cold the first night."**
   - **The Ancient One** stands and tells the other three Grandmasters, in two or three sentences, that **Drona has watched every house's instruments for a century**: "Drona lied." The room goes quiet in a new way.
   - **End:** the Master, rings off, looks at the band on the table, not at anyone, and says only, **"Prove it."** That sets up Ravi's paper in ch30.
