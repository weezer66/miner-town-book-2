## Beats
1. **The rule breaks, about 400 words.**
   - Rohan brings Elara to the dead room and **Nayan is already there**. It's the first time Elara has seen any of the four since the night they were introduced. Rohan says it plainly: the slow way ended with Baju, and so does this rule.
   - Nayan's first line to Elara is clipped and practical. **One beat for Baju:** his brass pin is still crooked in the fourth panel, and nobody has straightened it.
2. **Four sets of books, about 1,400 words.** This is the heart of the chapter.
   - The question that starts it: the Annex, the doubled library money and the mandate review all came out of Kubera's purse. "What if the hand isn't the only one spending other people's money?" Or Nayan's own question, from six years of copying: "I've never once put them next to each other."
   - **The physical act:** six years of dated pencil copies of all four houses' public books, laid edge to edge across the table and the floor. **Four columns of paper.** Shavings tin, pencils, the true flame at night.
   - They're looking for the hand's gifts, and they find those. Then **Nayan finds something else**: small purchases, regular for years, **in all four books at once**.
     - Each house's books show it buying for itself, for ordinary things: generator parts, filter media, medical stock, paper.
     - The amounts are small, the timing matches across the houses, and each line is approved by a founder-tier line no clerk signs.
     - **No house's books show the goods arriving anywhere a house would use them.**
     - "Every house thinks it's the one doing the buying."
   - **This is not the hand.** It's older than the hand (six years back and further, by the shape of it), steady, and it never grows. The hand's gifts grow; this doesn't. Elara sees the difference: "The hand feeds. This one eats. Just enough. For a very long time."
   - **Elara's dread is the review's ask from ch20.** She can't put the grey wall on this table, but she feels the shape of the blank location field. **Don't connect the two out loud.** At most one unspoken flicker.
   - **Dramatic irony:** the reader knows from ch6 that this is the ring. **Elara mustn't guess Patal Loka.** No name, no "ring," no "beneath." She only knows something that isn't a house has been fed by all four for a long time.
   - **Rohan goes still:** this is ground that has already been walked. "A path that feels like your own thinking arriving ahead of you." The Dhammapala's words, **verbatim from ch16.** "We don't chase it. We mark the place."
   - **Kaan's report** comes through Rohan, by paper: one line. *The ear has spoken to the analyst. He kept the appointment.* Ravi holds an old file and won't say what's in it. Keep it oblique; **Plan C is intact.**
3. **Rohan coda, about 600 words.** Rohan's POV, the Dhammapala, the lamp room.
   - He brings her the marked place: the four columns, the small steady line. **He doesn't bring the paper itself;** he brings what it shows.
   - She listens with her eyes closed. When she opens them: "You did not chase it." "No." "Good."
   - She tells him only this: there's one room where the four Grandmasters sit together, and **it has been asked to sit out of season** (by the Ancient One, in ch22). She'll carry this there. "It will be answered."
   - Rohan: "By whom?" / "By whoever has been eating, if the balance is kind." Keep her opaque, and **never say Patal Loka.**
   - **The door warning echo:** he's tired and sore. She notices and says one line about the door that is eager to break.
   - **End:** she puts out the lamp with two fingers. Optionally, she adds without looking at him: "They will send someone. Someone who reads patterns." This is a soft hook to ch24 (Ben goes topside). Or end on the smoke.
