## Beats
1. **Gage: Camilla, about 1,000 words.**
   - **The same night.** The ring's people reach the Hub. Camilla goes with them, or is at the lift when they bring him up.
   - **He's alive, badly hurt** (a crushed leg; he may not walk the deep again). He's conscious enough for one line: dry, or a count, or "Clean?"
   - Camilla's answer must be **true**, because the pact is never to lie. "No. Not clean. It worked."
   - **The cost, named:** the pump house, and Miner Town's ration water.
   - **His hands:** let them close round hers, or open.
2. **The paper: Ravi, about 1,000 words. This is Plan C.**
   - **The next day, topside.** Kaan's "Tomorrow. Bring what you took." This time Ravi brings the folio, to Elara, through Kaan.
   - Ravi is 20, three years in the analyst track. He's the swing piece (D10) and **chooses.**
   - **The file's meaning, revealed:**
     - his first-year report flagged a Kubera transfer signed with **two letters**, OG, three years ago;
     - it was sealed above his grade;
     - it's dated **before Daren ever wore the band.**
   - It's paper the city can't unsay: proof the Master wore it. "The record should say who sat on it" (his ch1 objection) pays off here.
   - **He hands it over and loses his standing** in the Annex he never wrote. **He chooses that.** One beat with the black king.
3. **The table, again: Elara, about 1,000 words. The deadlock.**
   - The four Grandmasters sit with the folio, the band, Baju's notebook, the register and Nayan's columns all on the table: **the paper road, complete.**
   - **And they can't agree** what to do with the Master. Kubera won't unseat its own. Drona's century of watching is out, so no house trusts Drona's word. The ring's leak is known. Ashoka holds the door and won't act alone.
   - **The balance is broken.** That's the Book 3 hook. The Master walks out of the room, still Master, rings back on, and every person there knows what he is.
   - **Elara and the Ancient One:** one exchange. Elara asks the question she was once told not to ask, and gets a true answer. Or the Ancient One gives her the next charge.
   - **Daren as witness (D7):** he testified, on the record, in the open. He has lost everything that was left. **Rohan and Daren, one beat:** Rohan doesn't forgive him and doesn't strike.
4. **The handoff: Daren or Camilla, about 600 words.**
   - **The de-keyed band ends up on Camilla's workbench** (structure §2), with Flitzberg's consent or the Grandmasters' failure to agree on anything else.
   - She doesn't work with anything she isn't allowed to take apart. **She looks at it beside her own arm: two halves.**
   - The listener on the shelf, wet and ruined, worked once.
5. **Four faces: Camilla, about 900 words.** The ending.
   - **The four together:**
     - Gage in the bed, his leg splinted;
     - Otto standing, very still;
     - Ben back from the screens;
     - Camilla.
   - **Book 1's epigraph answered:** four faces, **four looking back.** Make it land without stating it as a moral.
   - **Book 3 hooks, light and concrete, two or three at most:**
     - Miner Town's water: what the ration queue will look like tomorrow;
     - **Gretchen at the gates** on the screen, her worn patch, who will finally get word;
     - the crystal's blue quarter, **still steady blue**;
     - Elara above, bare-wristed at a window, and Camilla below, both watchers now;
     - the Master with his rings back on.
   - **Last line:** small, concrete, earned. One option is the bare-wrist image: Camilla or Elara choosing not to buckle a terminal back on, or Gage's open hands. **Not an aphorism.**
