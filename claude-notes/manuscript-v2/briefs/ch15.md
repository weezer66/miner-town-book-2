## Beats (in order; the proportions are a guide)
1. **Training, as a montage: about 700 words.** Show one concrete piece each, without technical detail:
   - **Camilla** works the arm with the engineer. The half-breath lag is shrinking. It's down to something she can count, a third of a breath. She hates that she likes it. One small, physical failure: she crushes something she meant to hold.
   - **Gage** walks a dark corridor in the ring with one hand on the wall, counting props that aren't there. It's his old run from the Hub to the junction, from memory.
   - **Ben** sits with the man at the screens and learns how Trinity's own watch changes at shift's end. He's good at it, and he doesn't like how good he is.
2. **The rehearsal: about 900 words.**
   - The three of them run the ten minutes on Gage's model with a sand-glass or a counted clock, with Flitzberg or the engineer as the "ring" that answers on Camilla's call.
   - **Keep the mission at story level.** Show roles, timing and nerves: Camilla calls it, Gage counts, Ben watches the "shift". No technical method. Valves are only "the feeds", answered on her word.
   - Each run goes wrong in a new human way, never a technical one: a miscount, a hesitation, Camilla calling it a beat early.
   - **The pattern Camilla sees:** every time, Gage's finger ends at the split splinter, at the junction, where *someone* has to be. They don't talk about it. She calls the last run clean, and it is, and that's worse.
   - **Don't resolve who goes to the junction.**
3. **Ben reads the blue quarter: about 700 words.**
   - In the room with the crystal, Ben counts its blue pulse the way he counts faces. It isn't random. It slows and quickens in a long cycle, warm and cool.
   - Camilla realises before he says it: **it keeps time with Otto's tank.** Or with Otto's breath. Leave which one open.
   - Nobody explains it, and Flitzberg (if present) admits he doesn't know. **Never technologize it. It stays unexplained.**
   - **Optional, approved seed:** Ben has been looking at Flitzberg's wall of the four houses' books. Every leak bends around one point no book shows. Flitzberg calls it "an old astronomers' lesson: a black hole, found only by the way light bends around what cannot be seen." Two or three lines at most, without naming anyone.
4. **Otto's line moves once: about 600 words.**
   - Ben's hour at the tank, with Camilla in the doorway. Ben talks to Otto the way he does: a sentence, then a gap left for a reply.
   - On the screen, Otto's flat line moves. **Once.** A single small rise in the quiet, then flat again.
   - Ben stops mid-sentence. Camilla saw it. The medic didn't, or did and logs it in silence.
   - **End:** Ben starts the sentence again, leaves the gap the same length, and waits. Nothing more happens. Camilla goes to find Gage, or doesn't, because she has to decide whether to tell him tonight. End on her choice or her silence.
   - **No finger movement.** That's saved for later.
