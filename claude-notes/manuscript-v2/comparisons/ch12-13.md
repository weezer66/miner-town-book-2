# Comparison: Chapters 12 and 13 (Opus vs Fable)

## Ch12 "In the Blue (I)", NOW · Otto (target 1,300)

| | Opus | Fable |
|---|---|---|
| Body words | about 1,350 | about 1,430 |
| Register | contractions; Otto's voice | formal "did not / could not" throughout, which is wrong for a 17-year-old |
| Voices | radio-mending voice (Ben) plus a silent sitter (Gage) | a sentence, then a gap left for a reply (Ben); "it was raining" |
| Stones / promise | the fourth stone that wanted to turn; "weighed exactly what one more stone would weigh" | the swallowed nosebleed (invented); "on purpose, and done it well" |
| Kalinga | "The last place I stood watch." | "The last place I stood." / "A door." / "Breathe, Otto." Lifted left big toe |
| Dry flicker | "Nice work, Otto-mobile. Top of the class." | "the one stance you couldn't stand up out of" |
| Ending | Kalinga as "an eleventh stone" | "He could not lift it. He had not stopped trying." |

**Merge:** Opus is the base, for its register, its Book 1 nickname and its stronger image system (the stones). Five things come from Fable:
- the dog-and-whistle line about the pull in his arms;
- Ben's voice as a sentence with a reply-sized gap, plus "He could have said it was raining";
- Shu Chan's lifted left big toe during the stance;
- "A door." added to the partial Kalinga answer;
- "He hadn't stopped trying." as the last line, and the gap-without-reply as the voice leaving.

**Result:** 1,501 words in all, about 1,415 of body (+9%). The butterfly appears only on the windowsill, and no finger moves.

## Ch13 "The Guardians", THEN · Rohan (target 3,600)

| | Opus | Fable |
|---|---|---|
| Body words | about 3,700 | about 3,930 |
| Tavi's question | "Line up. Bow out." He can't answer, as the brief asks | "Everyone loses, Tavi…", so he does answer, which loses the cut |
| Tests | live; Vayun corrects Rohan's whispered readings | same device |
| Statues | Ashoka (Kalinga), Cincinnatus, Leonidas, Lakshmibai, Masashige, with a cadet memory | Tecumseh instead of Leonidas; Masashige read against the obedience maxim |
| Student seed | no pronoun; the laugh stops when he says it | "He went below…": a pronoun, and the laugh comes after |
| Question back | asked; not answered | asked; not answered |
| Odd details | the inside-out sock; the brass-fish hairpin; Vayun eats the sweet first | Claudia's tape horn, found later on Rohan's sleeve; door handles worn to thumbs |

**Merge:** Opus is the base. It follows the brief on Tavi and keeps the student seed cleaner. Six grafts come from Fable:
- Claudia's tape-horn headguard and Rohan finding the horn on his sleeve;
- "Override was a procedural crime with a procedural answer…";
- "They climbed on him because he had never once made them afraid of it";
- "a console in a room with no galleries… no one to lay anything across her arms";
- "He doubted he was worth the door";
- the bronze handles worn to the shape of thumbs.

I cut Opus's upside-down-ribbon general to make room.

**Result:** 3,967 words in all, about 3,835 of body (+6.5%).

**Flags for the author:**
- Fable kept the Grandmaster's white hair from the source. Opus doesn't describe her hair, and the merge follows Opus.
- Both drafts have the Dhammapala already know that Rohan "decided to help Elara". This follows the source; Ch10's room register supports it.
