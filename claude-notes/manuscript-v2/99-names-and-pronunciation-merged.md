# Names and Pronunciation

*Many names in this book come from Sanskrit, Pali and Hindi, and a few from Japanese and Chinese. Here is how to say them. The syllable in capitals carries the stress. Say* uh *as in* about*,* ah *as in* father*,* ee *as in* see*,* oo *as in* boot*,* ay *as in* day*,* oh *as in* go*. An* h *after a consonant (*dh*,* th*) is a small puff of breath.*

## The houses and the city

**Ashoka** (uh-SHOH-kuh): the house of guardians, named for the emperor who laid down his sword.

**Drona** (DROH-nuh): the house of monitors and records, named for the great teacher.

**Kubera** (koo-BAY-ruh): the house of the treasury, named for the keeper of wealth.

**Chanakya** (chah-NUK-yuh): the house of analysts and archives, named for the old strategist.

**Meru** (MAY-roo): the tier above the cities, named for the mountain at the centre of the world.

**Sākṣī** (SAHK-shee): "witness".

**gana** (GUH-nuh): "group"; a house's working division.

**Patal Loka** (PAH-tahl LOH-kuh): from the old stories, the world beneath the world.

**Kalinga** (kuh-LING-guh): an old place name, and an old war.

## Titles

**Dhamma** (DHUM-muh): duty; the law a person keeps above any order.

**Dhammapala** (dhum-muh-PAH-luh): "keeper of Dhamma".

**Arthashastri** (uhr-thuh-SHAHS-tree): "master of statecraft".

**Sarvajña** (suhr-VUG-yuh): "the all-knowing".

**Draṣṭā** (DRUSH-tah): "the seer".

## Old stories

**Ramayana** (rah-MAH-yuh-nuh): the epic of Prince Rama.

**Sanjeevani** (sun-JEE-vuh-nee): the herb in the Ramayana that brings the dying back.

**Trishanku** (trih-SHUNG-koo): the king left hanging between heaven and earth.

**Ashwatthama** (ush-vut-THAH-mah): the warrior whose war never ends.

**Rahu** (RAH-hoo): the shadow that swallows the sun.

**graha** (GRUH-huh): "that which seizes".

**Vedas** (VAY-duhz): the oldest sacred books.

**karma** (KUHR-muh): the shape a choice takes after it leaves you.

**Rani Lakshmibai** (RAH-nee luk-shmee-BAH-ee): the warrior queen of Jhansi.

**Kusunoki Masashige** (koo-soo-NOH-kee mah-sah-SHEE-geh): a samurai remembered for his loyalty.

## Everyday words

**gurukul** (goo-ROO-kool): a school where students live with their teacher.

**dal** (DAHL): lentils.

**dojo** (DOH-joh): a training hall.

**sensei** (SEN-say): teacher.

**dan** (DAHN): a black-belt rank.

**kimono** (kih-MOH-noh): a training robe.

**Wadō-ryū** (WAH-doh RYOO): "the way of harmony", a school of karate.
