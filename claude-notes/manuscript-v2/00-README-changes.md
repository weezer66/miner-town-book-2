## 01 · Then · Elara · "Assigned to Witness" (~2,000 words; original Ch1 was 2,400)
**Built from:** original Ch1, mostly your sentences, tightened.

**What changed:**
- **Bare-wrist seed (new, opening):** her terminal sits on the shelf overnight; the room is quiet until she buckles it on and "the city began to talk." It sets up Ch24's "It feels like going deaf," and it's consistent with Ch24 ("She had taken hers off before, of course — to wash, to sleep").
- **Ages (D9):** "Four of them, and not one older than twenty-one. Trinity staffed its Initiation watch with new graduates on purpose…"
- **One human detail per monitor:**
  - Ravi: the cuff script she glimpses but can't read (it's "Meaning precedes action," Ch13).
  - Daren: touches his gold insignia twice, a hint of the insecurity behind his redemption arc.
  - Rohan: split, healing knuckles (from catching the Little Dragons' fists, Ch15; Elara only guesses).
- **Gage: 280 → 220 lb** (Book 1 canon).
- **Book 1 nod:** her pulse settles "toward sixty-five, the number the towers liked their watchers to hold." Book 1 had Elara held at 65 bpm.
- **World paragraph moved** to the Skylark ride, as the city falls away beneath her, so it no longer arrives before we've met anyone. The Meru "merely" line is kept.
- **Catching Ravi's wrist** now costs her a beat: his pulse, hers, "somewhere a sensor had written it down."
- **Constructions you'd banned are gone:**
  - "Not dramatic rebellion. Just absence." → "She didn't picture rebellion. She pictured absence…"
  - "Not *Are you healthy?*…" → "The plate wasn't asking whether…"
- **Contractions** in Elara's dialogue ("It's our first day… they'll never stop supervising us").
- **Cut:** most of the station-doctrine explanations (one line each now), "In Trinity, altitude measured distance from other people's pain," and the second-guess paragraph about Ravi's possessiveness.
- **Ending:** still Otto as "a source," then "He is mine to size up." The plan said to end on "Assign Gage to me"; I kept your original order because the Otto line is a stronger last beat, and Gage is claimed two pages earlier. Swap them if you prefer.

## 02 · Now · Camilla · "The Journey Ahead" (~2,700 words)
**Built from:** original Ch19, first half (Flitzberg's POV), retold from inside Camilla's head, picking up **exactly** at Book 1's last page.

**What changed:**
- **Opens on Camilla coming back to consciousness:** the count first, then the cold floor, Ben's spray can, and the **phantom left arm**. She recognizes it from Gerald (Book 1 Ch15, the three questions over Refined Mash).
- **Book 1's final exchange, verbatim:**
  - "Why? … What did we ever do to you?"
  - the birthright line;
  - "What does that mean?";
  - "Four faces. Three looking back.";
  - "You are ready now… Ready for the journey ahead."
- **Camilla checks Flitzberg's jaw for a seam** (Book 1: the borrowed faces). He's wearing his own face: smooth pale skin, full cheeks. This is the Book 1 description, not "an old man."
- **Camilla's agency (new):** she works out why Flitzberg came in alone. Then she says **"Gage. … Stop."**, calling in Book 1 Ch22's promise ("If I tell you to stop, you stop"). Her word is what opens his hand. The lead acts from a stretcher.
- **"Not yet."** She refuses to be wheeled out until he answers about Otto, so she can witness the whole scene. The medics treat her in the room.
- **The stones line is fixed:** "Flitzberg looked at Otto — the boy who had held ten stones…" (The original was ambiguous between Ben and Otto.)
- **Camilla pushes back** on being made a story: "Don't make it a story. I didn't meet anything. I counted." It's a teen voice, and it rejects the adult's framing.
- **Pappy callback:** "If you can read the manual, you own the machine" (Book 1), set against the deeds and charters.
- **Exit image (new):** Gage lifts Otto across his shoulders, and Camilla sees the mine gates again (Book 1 Ch6). "This time Otto didn't know it."
- **The crystal:** three quarters green, the last quarter "a cold, slow blue, like something that hadn't been decided yet." That's the Book 1 open thread.
- **Closing line:** "Four of them through the door. Three of them awake. It was a count she didn't know how to finish."
- **Register:** teens use contractions; Flitzberg stays formal (no contractions) as contrast; the medic is warm and casual.
- **Moved to later chapters, from the original Ch19:** Flitzberg's internal reasoning about why he walked in alone (Camilla now infers it in one paragraph), the Ravi celebration section, and the Rohan/Dhammapala lamp scene.
