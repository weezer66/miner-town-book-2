## What the test says about the models
- **Fable 5.1:** richer sensory texture, more surprising specific details, and the deepest Book 1 recall. But it carried over an existing continuity error from the source, and it followed the brief's tightening and prose rules a bit less strictly. It was also much slower (about 10 minutes for two chapters).
- **Opus 5.5:** tighter, follows the brief and the continuity rules closely, and makes stronger structural moves (Opus-A's opening and ending). Its prose is clean but plainer.
- **The two runs of Opus differed from each other** as much as Opus differed from Fable. Some of the variation is just the draw.

**Suggested workflow for the remaining chapters:** Fable drafts the scenes. Opus does a canon, continuity and brief-compliance pass on each draft (Book 1 facts, the decision log, the banned constructions) and proposes structural grafts. You choose.
