One word

Rules

Tamil script always uses Tamil rules; Devanagari uses Hindi or Sanskrit as chosen. Hindi drops unwritten short a sounds (नयन → nayan); Sanskrit, Pali and Tamil keep every vowel. In Latin input a plain a is short; write aa/ā for the long one, ee for ई, oo for ऊ, and in Tamil zh for ழ, th for த. Script input is more reliable than Latin.

Consonants worth knowing

  • kh th phख थ फa puff of breath after the consonant
  • thதin Tamil, a plain t with the tongue on the teeth
  • zhழbetween l and r, tongue curled back; most readers say l
  • CAPSthe stressed syllable

Stress is approximate: Hindi stress is light and weight-based, Tamil leans on the first syllable. Vowel length and quality are what English readers get wrong, and those are what the verdicts check. The voices are your browser's own, so a Hindi or Tamil voice gives the truest reading of the word; Hindi voices drop a final a even in Sanskrit mode, where the suggested guide is the better reference. In Tamil, consonants soften between vowels and after nasals (கம்பன் → Kamban); the tool applies that. Dental and retroflex t/d are both written t/d.
